# Galok — Look closer

Galok is an independent visual archive following cities, prices, platforms and ordinary life through photographs, moving image, writing and data.

The work begins at street level. Beijing, Shanghai, Xi'an and Xiamen become four open field notes: architecture, transit, weather, shopfronts, gestures and small decisions held in the same frame. The archive then moves outward. Works keeps design and moving-image experiments. Notes keeps observations and process fragments. Data follows the longer lines behind the essays. Archive makes the whole collection browsable.

One receipt can hold an economy. One street can show the rules. One photograph can keep a question open.

Galok works at three distances: view the pressure, frame the evidence, observe the scene.
