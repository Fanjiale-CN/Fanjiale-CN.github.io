# Minimal Zine Poster v0.1 prompt

Create a tall vertical 3:5 poetic minimal paper poster, full-frame warm aged ivory paper with no border and no mockup. Keep 78–85% of the canvas as quiet empty paper. Place one compact visual cluster in the lower-middle, occupying about 16% of the canvas, with generous air on every side.

The subject is a small archival city-grid specimen: a simplified street intersection seen from above, assembled from two torn grayscale map fragments and one tiny receipt strip, suggesting that an ordinary city detail can reveal a larger system. Treat the grayscale fragments with xerox softness, visible torn fibers, restrained halftone, slight scan misregistration, and old photocopy wear.

Use sparse typewriter and small serif typography integrated into the cluster. The only clearly readable phrase is “LOOK CLOSER.” in small uppercase type, with tiny semi-legible archive coordinates and “FIELD NOTE 01” nearby. Add one unmistakably fully saturated tomato-red risograph rectangle crossing part of the street grid, occupying about 1.8% of the whole canvas and roughly 25% of the cluster. Preserve the red saturation despite paper grain and ink bleed. Use subdued gray-black for every other mark.

Flat orthographic scanned-paper appearance, matte absorbent paper, diffuse light, low-to-medium contrast, quiet archival editorial mood, independent Japanese/Korean zine sensibility, distant and memory-like. Avoid full-bleed scenes, commercial headline hierarchy, logo, CTA, brand campaign styling, glossy mockup, clean digital UI, cinematic lighting, 3D, hard shadows, neon, gradients, cute illustration, fashion drama, dense scrapbook, multiple bright colors, long text, and photorealistic stock imagery.
