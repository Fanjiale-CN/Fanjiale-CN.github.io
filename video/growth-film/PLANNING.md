# Growth Film / Production Plan

## Product brief

- **Surface:** muted, looping hero film inside `galok.me/design/`.
- **Job:** demonstrate the design language instead of merely describing it.
- **Audience:** readers encountering Galok's visual system for the first time.
- **Promise:** Galok is a personal magazine and city memory book that looks slowly, edits deliberately and keeps evidence findable.
- **Constraint:** silent autoplay, 1920×1080, 30fps, 38 seconds, paper / ink / signal red only.

## Decisions

| Question | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Audio | None. The page is an editorial field, not a trailer. |
| Imagery | Real Galok city and data imagery, never fabricated interface replicas. |
| Motion | One primary motion grammar per shot; ease-out; no bounce, glint or elastic scale. |
| Palette | `#f7f4ef`, `#171717`, `#9e2a2b`, `#d8d2c8`, `#77736b`. |
| Type | Floane for editorial display, Gambetta for reading, mono-like tracking for metadata. |
| Loop | Final archive mark yields to the opening paper field without an audible or visual hit. |

## Video Shotcraft recipe use

| Sequence | Relationship to the recipe library |
| --- | --- |
| Opening | Direct adaptation of `brand-ink-open`, using the real Galok mark and a paper crosshair. |
| Three lenses | Direct adaptation of `word-relay-filmstrip`: the card moves by exactly one measured height only when the verb changes. |
| Archive merge | Direct adaptation of `bezier-source-converge-merge`: staggered paths draw, nodes travel, sources are absorbed and paths erase. |
| Frame scan | Custom Shotcraft composition informed by scan-and-annotate grammar. It deliberately omits a diagnostic UI and scan-speed changes. |
| Observe | Custom Shotcraft composition informed by spotlight grammar. It uses one physical aperture and omits glints and UI-card choreography. |
| Outro | Custom Shotcraft composition informed by strip-away grammar. Editorial lens plates replace product UI and the ending returns to the exact first frame for a seamless loop. |

## Storyboard

1. A measured crosshair locates the Galok mark. It arrives as ink, not as a logo sting.
2. A red seed interrupts a hairline. One drawn root becomes a stem and a small field. The sentence is direct: growth first; the field sets the form.
3. Five city photographs move by exactly one card height while three verbs change at the same beat: view, frame, observe.
4. A scan crosses a real Hangzhou frame. Only after the scan passes do crop brackets and a note appear: a crop is a claim.
5. A quiet aperture visits three real scenes, settles on the middle photograph, then gives it a small physical lift. Observation is time, not effect.
6. City, image, writing and data follow curved paths into the actual Galok mark. Their paths erase after absorption.
7. Three lens panels and the index strip leave from the outside inward. The archive field and Galok remain for a full final hold.

## Accessibility and review gates

- No audio stream, flashing, rapid cuts or semantic text below 32px.
- Main captions are 60–118px and remain readable for at least one second.
- No scene relies on color alone; labels accompany the red signal.
- Source images remain recognizable and are treated with grayscale / ink contrast only.
- Reduced-motion is handled by the surrounding page; the film is decorative and muted.
