METHOD / PROVENANCE

What is actually in the frame.

All values are annual percentage changes from the World Bank’s World Development Indicators. GDP, household consumption and capital formation use constant-price national-accounts series. CPI comes from the IMF International Financial Statistics database through the same World Bank release.

The common window ends in 2024 because household-consumption data for 2025 is not yet available in this release. The red 2020–2023 band is Galok’s editorial marker for the disruption period; it is not an official statistical classification.
