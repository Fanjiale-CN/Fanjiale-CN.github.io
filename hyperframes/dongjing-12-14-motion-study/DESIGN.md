# Dongjing 11–14 motion study

## Style Prompt

A restrained editorial motion study on warm archival paper. The camera logic continuously narrows scale: district clock, night network, wine-house section, table-side service ecology. Hairline geometry and large bilingual typography reveal evidence; nothing imitates an antique interface. Motion feels measured and documentary, never theatrical or nostalgic.

## Colors

- Paper: `#f2ede0`
- Ink: `#171713`
- Signal red: `#a22d28`
- Jade: `#254f47`
- Night: `#151715`

## Typography

- Display Chinese: Galok Qiji / project Qiji subset
- Primary Chinese: Galok Reading Serif TW / project GenRyu subset
- English display: existing Galok sans-serif system
- Metadata: monospace

## What NOT to Do

- No scrolls, clouds, ink wash, smoke, seals as decoration or imperial ornament
- No neon, gradients, particles, glow fields or cinematic fantasy reconstruction
- No photoreal invented Song architecture or exact street map
- No infinite loops or random motion
- No menu-card montage for Entry 14

## Narrative arc

1. Entry 11: the market changes by time of day.
2. Entry 12: the district branches after dark.
3. Entry 13: the camera enters the building and reveals its distribution system.
4. Entry 14: the building resolves into one table surrounded by labor and trade.
