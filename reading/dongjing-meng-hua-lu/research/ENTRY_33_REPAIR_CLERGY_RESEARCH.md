Core proposition:

> **THE CITY'S REPAIR LABOR AND RITUAL LABOR POOL IN THE OPEN AIR EVERY MORNING, WAITING TO BE HIRED.**

Two needs — a house to repair and a memorial offering to hold — funnel into one morning scene. At the bridge markets and street-lane mouths, wood and bamboo craftsmen (`木竹匠人`, called `雜貨工匠`), general laborers (`雜作人夫`), Daoist priests and monks stand in rows, gathered and waiting for a call. Meng gives this standing pool a name: `羅齋`. Materials have their own shops (`鋪席`); bricklayers and plasterers come the moment they are wanted (`隨手即就`).

The page is a **morning hiring roster**: the roles standing at the bridge mouth, the two needs that summon them, and the supply channels that complete the job. It cross-references Entry 24 / 《雇覓人力》 for the intermediary layer that Entry 33 shows without middlemen.

## Working text

### 33A / THE MORNING POOL

> 倘欲修整屋宇，泥補牆壁，生辰忌日，欲設齋僧尼道士，即早辰橋市街巷口皆有木竹匠人，謂之雜貨工匠，以至雜作人夫，道士僧人，羅立會聚，候人請喚，謂之「羅齋」。竹木作料，亦有鋪席。磚瓦泥匠，隨手即就。

## Secure source-derived structure

- Two trigger needs: repairing a house / patching walls (`修整屋宇，泥補牆壁`), and holding a vegetarian offering on birthdays or death-days (`生辰忌日，欲設齋僧尼道士`).
- Time marker: `早辰` — early morning. This is the entry's only temporal marker; the practice is otherwise undated.
- Place marker: `橋市街巷口` — the mouths of bridge markets, streets and lanes.
- The standing roles: `木竹匠人` (wood and bamboo craftsmen, called `雜貨工匠`), `雜作人夫` (general laborers), `道士僧人` (Daoist priests and monks).
- `羅立會聚，候人請喚` — they stand arrayed and gathered, waiting to be called. Meng names this scene `羅齋`.
- Supply channels: `竹木作料，亦有鋪席` (bamboo and timber materials have their own shops); `磚瓦泥匠，隨手即就` (bricklayers and plasterers come at once).

## Glossary (page scope)

- `雜貨工匠` — the text's own term for the wood and bamboo craftsmen.
- `雜作人夫` — general laborers.
- `羅齋` — the text-defined name for the gathered morning pool of craftsmen, laborers and clergy awaiting hire.
- `鋪席` — shop establishments (here, for bamboo and timber materials).
- `隨手即就` — "done the moment you reach for them": immediate availability.
- `設齋` — holding a vegetarian offering; the occasion terms `生辰` (birthday) and `忌日` (death-day) are kept as transmitted.

## Collation / textual uncertainty

- Title/body wording difference: the title prints `齋僧請道` while the body prints `設齋僧尼道士`. Both are preserved; the page does not harmonize them.
- `早辰` (not `早晨`) — confirmed across the Siku witness and both modern transcriptions.
- Siku orthographic variants: `墻` for `牆`, `朩` for `木`, `㑹` for `會`.

## Unresolved

- Whether `羅齋` also implies a hiring fee or standing arrangement. The text records the scene and the name only; the page does not infer a price mechanism.
