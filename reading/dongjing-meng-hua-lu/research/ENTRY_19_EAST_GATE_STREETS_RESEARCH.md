## Secure source-derived structure

### 19A / EAST-GATE FRONTAGE

> 寺東門大街，皆是襆頭、腰帶、書籍、冠朵鋪席，丁家素茶。寺南即錄事巷妓館。繡巷皆師姑繡作居住。北即小甜水巷，巷內南食店甚盛，妓館亦多。向北李慶糟薑鋪。

Secure reading:

- The east-gate street has shops for headwear, belts, books and head ornaments, plus a named tea seller.
- South and north side lanes add entertainment, embroidery work and a strong southern-food presence.
- The text names a preserved-ginger shop farther north.

Treat this as adjacency and textual sequence. Do not assign parcel boundaries, equal distances or modern coordinates.

### 19B / NORTH, THEN TURN EAST

> 直北出景靈宮東門前。又向北曲東稅務街、高頭街、薑行後巷，乃脂皮畫曲妓館。南北講堂巷、孫殿丞藥鋪、靴店。出界北巷，巷口宋家生藥鋪，本鋪中兩壁皆李成所畫山水。

Secure reading:

- The route goes north toward Jingling Palace's east gate, then bends east.
- Named streets / lanes, entertainment, medicine and boot shops appear in close succession.
- At the mouth of Jiebei Lane, Meng explicitly says both walls inside the Song-family crude-drug shop carried landscapes painted by Li Cheng.

### Li Cheng limit

This is strong evidence for elite visual culture inside a commercial interior.

It does **not** prove:

- that the paintings were for sale;
- that the pharmacy functioned as a gallery;
- who commissioned the paintings;
- when they were painted;
- that this was a general practice for pharmacies.

The visual module may evoke landscape painting abstractly, but should not fabricate a surviving shop interior.

### 19C / EASTWARD TO THE ANCESTRAL TEMPLE

> 自景靈宮東門大街向東，街北舊乾明寺，沿火改作五寺三監。以東向南曰第三條甜水巷，以東熙熙樓客店，都下著數。以東街南高陽正店，向北入馬行。向東，街北曰車輅院，南曰第二甜水巷。以東審計院，以東桐樹子韓家，直抵太廟前門。南往觀音院，乃第一條甜水巷也。太廟北入榆林巷，通曹門大街，不能遍數也。

Secure reading:

- Religious / institutional reuse, a notable guesthouse, a principal wine house, carriage administration, the audit office, the three Sweetwater lanes, the ancestral temple and Caomen Street all enter the same eastward textual sequence.
- `都下著數` supports cautious wording such as “counted among the capital's notable establishments” for Xixi Tower guesthouse.
- The final `不能遍數也` is editorially important: the urban catalogue acknowledges that it cannot enumerate everything.

## Translation policy

Keep proper names visible where identification is uncertain.

Avoid over-modernizing:

- `妓館` beyond “courtesan / entertainment houses” without a note;
- `五寺三監` into a modern ministry complex;
- `車輅院` into a generic transport department without institutional research;
- `正店` into a modern bar / chain restaurant.

Keep direction words as relative textual directions: north, turn east, eastward, south / north of named anchors.
