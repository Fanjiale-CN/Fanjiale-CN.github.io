# Entry 31 — 《皇后出乘輿》 research / text lock

Status: TEXT LOCK for `feature/dongjing-volume-iv-31-34`

## Editorial role

Entry 31 is Volume IV / 4 OF 12, the last court-facing entry before the volume turns to rentals and restaurants.

Batch arc:

`COURT IN MOTION → THE RENTAL CITY`

Public working title:

**RIDING OUT, BY RANK**

Core proposition:

> **THE EMPRESS'S VEHICLE DEFINES THE TOP OF A LADDER THE WHOLE CITY CAN RENT INTO.**

Meng does not describe one vehicle. He defines a term (`輿`), differentiates it from the wedding palanquin of Entry 30 (`檐子`), and then descends to the ox-drawn `坐車子` ordinary titled women and commoners ride. The final turn of the entry is commercial: every rung of the ladder, including attendants' clothing, is available through rental (`假賃`).

The page is a **vehicle ladder / rental spine**: rank, ornament, entourage and draught differentiate the vehicles; rental makes the system circulate.

## Working text

### 31A / THE TERM AND THE ENTOURAGE

> 皇太后、皇后出乘者，謂之「輿」。比檐子稍增廣，花樣皆龍，前後簷皆剪棕，儀仗與駕出相似而少，仍無駕頭、警蹕耳。

### 31B / THE LADDER DESCENDS, AND RENTS

> 士庶家與貴家婚嫁，亦乘檐子，只無脊上銅鳳花朵，左右兩軍，自有假賃所在。以至從人衫帽衣服從物俱可賃，不須借。餘命婦王宮士庶通乘坐車子，如檐子樣制，亦可容六人，前後有小勾欄，底下軸貫兩挾朱輪，前出長轅約七八尺，獨牛駕之，亦可假賃。

## Secure source-derived structure

- The term `輿` is applied specifically to the empress dowager's and empress's vehicle (`皇太后、皇后出乘者，謂之「輿」`).
- The `輿` is slightly larger than a `檐子` (`稍增廣`), carries dragon ornament (`花樣皆龍`), and has trimmed palm on both front and rear canopies (`前後簷皆剪棕`).
- Its escort resembles the emperor's outing but is reduced (`儀仗與駕出相似而少`), and it lacks the `駕頭` and the `警蹕` — both presented in the text as emperor-specific marks.
- Wedding palanquins for commoner and noble families are `檐子` minus the ridge's copper phoenix ornament — the same ornament Entry 30 inventories on the princess's sedan (`渗金銅鑄雲鳳花朵`).
- `左右兩軍，自有假賃所在` — the text asserts rental depots exist; the institutional referent of `兩軍` is not spelled out in the entry.
- Attendants' shirts, hats, clothing and accessories can all be rented; there is no need to borrow.
- The `坐車子` follows the `檐子` pattern, seats six, has small railings fore and aft, an axle through two red wheels, long shafts of about seven to eight `尺` extending forward, and is drawn by a single ox. It too can be rented.

## Glossary (page scope)

- `輿` — the palanquin term reserved in this entry for the empress dowager / empress.
- `檐子` — the shoulder-borne palanquin; Entry 30 documents its construction in detail.
- `坐車子` — the ox-drawn two-wheel cart for titled women, palace families and commoners.
- `駕頭` — a processional object reserved to the imperial train; described on the page only as "the imperial procession marker", without further specification.
- `警蹕` — the clearing-and-warning protocol of an imperial movement (出警入蹕); again reserved to the imperial train.
- `命婦` — women holding court rank titles.
- `假賃` — rental / hire service; the operative commercial word of Entries 31–34.

## Chronology / evidence status

- The entry carries **no date**. It is a remembered, standing description of vehicle practice, not an event.
- Memory horizon (site-established dates reused from Entries 28–30): Meng in the capital from 1103; capital lost 1127; book completed 1147.
- `輿` / `檐子` / `坐車子` distinctions: TEXT / CONFIRMED.
- `駕頭` / `警蹕` as emperor-reserved marks: LIKELY (the text itself frames them as the items the empress's train lacks; institutional detail kept minimal).
- `左右兩軍` referent: UNCERTAIN — text reading preserved; no institutional identification asserted.

## Collation / textual uncertainty

- `檐子` vs `簷子`: the Siku-family witness prints `檐`; the Gutenberg-family transcription prints `簷`. The site keeps `檐`, consistent with Entries 29–30.
- `不須借措`: the Siku witness prints `不須借措`; the punctuated working text ends the sentence at `不須借`. The extra `措` is preserved in the page's collation trail as an unresolved trailing character (possible dittograph or damaged reading), not silently harmonized.
- Siku orthographic variants in this entry: `乗` for `乘`, `㡌` for `帽`, `様製` for `樣制`, `㯶` for `棕`.
- `通乘坐車子` is punctuated as transmitted; no emendation proposed.

## Unresolved

- The institutional meaning of `左右兩軍` (guard-command rental depots is the natural reading given Entry 28's uniformed services and Entry 30's `天武官抬舁`, but the page does not assert it).
- The disposition of the Siku `措` in `不須借措`.
