# Entry 52 — 清明節

## Working text lock

> 清明節，尋常京師以冬至後一百五日為大寒食。前一日謂之「炊熟」，用麵造棗䭅飛燕，柳條串之，插於門楣，謂之「子推燕」。子女及笄者，多以是日上頭。寒食第三節，即清明日矣。凡新墳皆用此日拜掃。都城人出郊。禁中前半月發宮人車馬朝陵，宗室南班近親，亦分遣詣諸陵墳享祀。從人皆紫衫、白絹三角子、青行纏，皆係官給。節日亦禁中出車馬，詣奉先寺道者院祀諸宮人墳，莫非金裝紺幰、錦額珠簾、繡扇雙遮、紗籠前導。士庶闐塞諸門，紙馬鋪皆於當街用紙衮疊成樓閣之狀。四野如市，往往就芳樹之下，或園囿之間，羅列盃盤，互相勸酬。都城之歌兒舞女，遍滿園亭。抵暮而歸，各攜棗䭅、炊餅、黃胖、掉刀、名花異果、山亭戲具、鴨卵雞雛，謂之「門外土儀」。轎子即以楊柳雜花裝簇頂上，四垂遮映。自此三日皆出城上墳，但一百五日最盛。節日坊市賣稠餳、麥糕、乳酪、乳餅之類。緩入都門，斜陽御柳；醉歸院落，明月梨花。諸軍禁衛各成隊伍，跨馬作樂四出，謂之「𣘚腳」。其旗旌鮮明，軍容雄壯，人馬精銳，又別為一景也。

## Collation / lexical lock

- **`為大寒食`** — LOCKED. Siku transmits the phrase continuously; 《歲時廣記》 quotes 《東京夢華錄》 as `京師以冬至後一百五日為大寒食`. Working punctuation is `為大寒食。前一日…`; reject the digital punctuation `為大。寒食前一日…`.
- **`棗䭅飛燕`** — Siku supplies `䭅`; Gutenberg drops it. Keep `棗䭅飛燕`.
- **`紺幰`** — keep Siku `幰`; reject OCR-like `闤`.
- **`斜陽御柳` / `斜楊御栁`** — working reading `斜陽御柳`, with Siku `斜楊` retained in apparatus. The paired prose `斜陽御柳；醉歸院落，明月梨花` strongly supports the working reading.
- **`𣘚腳 / 捽腳 / 摔腳`** — LOCKED AS VARIANT + GLOSS. Siku has `𣘚腳`; the NLC Ming witness OCR reads `捽腳`; many modern edited texts print `摔腳`. Fang Yizhi's 《通雅》 explicitly glosses `清明出遊曰𣘚腳` and cites the Song Qingming guard scene. Meaning can therefore be given cautiously as a **Qingming outing / going-out term**. Do not turn it into a specific dance, cavalry drill, or parade genre.
- **`黃胖`** — HIGH. Later Song evidence and lexicographic tradition identify it as a clay figurine / clay figure called *huangpang*.
- **`掉刀`** — HIGH as an attested weapon-name/form in military literature; MEDIUM for the hypothesis that the Qingming item is a toy/miniature. Main text should not silently convert it into a toy sword.
- **`山亭戲具`** — MEDIUM-HIGH as miniature/scenic playthings, supported by later vernacular lists of `山亭兒、庵兒、寶塔兒、石橋兒…`; mark the later date of the comparison.
- **`鴨卵雞雛`** — keep literal unless stronger evidence proves these are models or toys.

## Claim lock

HIGH:
- Qingming produces a mass outward movement through the city gates toward graves, suburban space and gardens.
- `士庶闐塞諸門` and `四野如市` are Meng's own crowd-language.
- Mourning rites, court tomb visitation, food, entertainment, flowers, market goods and return traffic occupy the same festival field.
- `門外土儀` is Meng's label for the goods brought back from outside the gates.
- Sedan chairs are explicitly decorated with willow and mixed flowers.

CAUTION:
- `四野如市` and `歌兒舞女遍滿園亭` are memoir-scale statements, not headcounts.
- The material bundle at return should be itemized with uncertainty rather than reconstructed as a modern souvenir market.

Editorial proposition: **THE CITY GOES OUT.**

# Entry 53 — 三月一日開金明池瓊林苑

## Core lock

The entry establishes the spatial system used by most of Volume VII: seasonal opening, Linshui Hall, Immortal Bridge, central halls, Baojin Tower, viewing structures, food, performance areas, trade, pawn activity, fishing, boat storage and guard display.

> 三月一日，州西順天門外開金明池、瓊林苑。每日教習車駕上池儀範，雖禁從，士庶許縱賞，御史臺有榜不得彈劾。

A near-contemporary comparison in Zhou Hui's 《清波雜志》 records a third-month-first-day order opening Jinming Pool, permitting commoners to visit and stating that the censorate should not impeach them. Use this as **COMPARATIVE** confirmation of seasonal permission, not as substitute text.

## Collation lock

- **`面北臨水殿 / 西北臨水殿`** — STRONG PREFERRED READING: `面北臨水殿` for working spatial reading and translation. Siku/Kanripo has `西北`; the NLC Ming printed witness reads `面北`; edited tradition also preserves `面北`; the south-bank / north-facing spatial syntax is coherent with architectural reconstruction. The Siku reading must remain visible in apparatus. Never state that archaeology alone proves the character.
- **`閉池 / 閑池`** — HIGH PREFERRED: `閉池`. Siku has `閑池`; the NLC Ming witness reads `閉池`; Gutenberg/edited tradition supports `閉池`; the MOE dictionary entry for `典沒` quotes the Dongjing sentence with `閉池`; Entry 60 explicitly gives `四月八日閉池`. Interpret the sentence as a pawn/redemption deadline tied to the pool season. Do not infer a citywide statute.
- **Dimensions** — remain Meng's reported measurements. Never convert them into an exact GIS footprint without a stated uncertainty model.

## Claim lock

HIGH:
- Jinming Pool/Qionglin Garden becomes a seasonally opened, selectively accessible landscape containing viewing, commerce, performance and leisure.
- East and west banks are described differently: the east side is denser with viewing structures and commerce; the west side is quieter and associated with fishing.
- Public access is layered. `士庶許縱賞` does not mean unrestricted access to every imperial space.
- Pawn activity is temporally tied in the narrative to the opened-pool season.

Editorial proposition: **THE TEMPORARY CITY.**

Visualization rule: use a **relational map** before an exact geometric reconstruction.

# Entry 54 — 駕幸臨水殿觀爭標錫宴

## Sequence lock

Preserve the procedural order:

1. Emperor at Linshui Hall; banquet for officials.
2. Water-stage / guard display.
3. Four colored boats carrying military entertainments.
4. Two music boats.
5. Water-puppet boat: fisherman, child rower, live fish, wooden-figure ball/dance acts.
6. Painted swing boats and `水鞦韆` dive.
7. Performance boats withdraw.
8. Twenty small dragon boats.
9. Ten tiger-head boats.
10. Two flying-fish boats.
11. Two single-person loach boats / dugouts.
12. Small boats pull the great dragon boat from the `奧屋`.
13. Red-flag signals cue movement.
14. `旋羅 → 海眼 → 交頭` formations.
15. Boats align before the halls.
16. `標竿` is set in the water.
17. Flag signal → simultaneous advance → winner takes the marker → acclaim / bowing.
18. **Three** contests; small boats return the great dragon boat to storage.

## Text / social lock

- **Race count `三次`** — HIGH. Siku and the NLC Ming witness both read `各三次爭標而止`; multiple edited witnesses agree. Do not use later `二次` readings without explicit variant framing.
- **`百姓卸在行人`** — TEXT HIGH. Quan Hansheng interprets `卸在行` as a guild/group of loading/unloading or porter laborers. This interpretation gains internal support from Volume I's warehouse vocabulary `支納下卸` / `下卸` and the description of specialized carrying labor, but a second unambiguous Song occurrence of the exact label has not been found. Status: **porter/loading-guild interpretation MEDIUM-HIGH; exact identity unresolved**. In main prose, attribute the interpretation or say “a group Meng calls 百姓卸在行人”; avoid bare `dockworkers` / `stevedores` as fact.
- Water-puppet and water-swing mechanisms remain undescribed beyond observable effects. No speculative machinery.

## Claim lock

HIGH:
- Repeated `旗招之` is an explicit visual control system.
- The boats form named patterns before the final contest.
- Puppetry, acrobatics, music, military personnel, civilian participants and imperial spectatorship coexist in the program.
- This belongs to the **lunar third-month Jinming Pool** outing sequence. Do not present it as a Duanwu festival race.

Editorial proposition: **THE SIGNAL.**

Best device: choreography / cue-sequence diagram with each `旗招之` as a control event.

# Entry 56 — 駕幸寶津樓宴殿

## Core structure

- banquet hall south of Baojin Tower;
- spaces ordinarily closed to general entry;
- shooting hall;
- ball / training ground;
- colored spectator stands;
- imperial appearance increases attendance.

## Text lock

- **`大撮焦亭子`** — LOCKED AS A PHRASE. Iriya Yoshitaka's review of Deng Zhicheng specifically identifies the old punctuation `大撮焦，亭子方池` as wrong and says `大撮焦亭子` must be read together. A 2025 CASS Institute of Philosophy article documents this correction and its adoption in the revised annotation tradition.
- **`焦 → 角`** — remains a conjectural semantic emendation in some modern annotation, usually explained as a pavilion with raised corners. It is **not** strong enough for silent normalization. Working text stays `大撮焦亭子`; translation can describe it conservatively as a named/technical pavilion term or leave the label visible.
- The old textual problem around `殿之南…` / possible extra directional character belongs in apparatus if the page uses a precise ground plan; it does not block the entry's access argument.

Editorial proposition: **THE EDGE OF ACCESS.**

Guardrail: the surrounding landscape is selectively open; the banquet/restricted zones remain bounded.

## Lexical / material lock

- **`抹蹌`** — HIGH as face-painting / powder-associated performance vocabulary; Meng himself describes yellow and white powder on the face, and lexicographic evidence supports the gloss.
- **`舞判`** — HIGH as a Judge/Zhong Kui-style dance/performance figure, matching the text's long beard, green robe, boots/tablet and gong accompaniment.
- **`丁都賽`** — excellent COMPARATIVE MATERIAL EVIDENCE. The National Museum of China holds a Northern Song theatrical brick inscribed `丁都賽` and explicitly connects the name with the performer recorded in 《東京夢華錄》. This supports the historical circulation of the name; it does **not** prove that the brick depicts the exact Baojin Tower performance.
- **`小打 / 大打`** — the text itself describes teams, mounted players, sticks, ball, gate/goal and scoring. Safe: “mounted stick-and-ball games/forms.” Avoid automatic one-to-one equation with modern polo.
- **`妙法院`** — institutional identity remains insufficiently secure. Describe only what the text gives: palace-associated riders, young women in male-style dress, riding, archery / weapon display and ceremonial movement. Do not label them a modern-style women’s polo team.
- **`硬鬼 / 七聖刀 / 歇帳` and other labels** — preserve historical labels with descriptive translation where the action is clear; do not invent stable modern genre equivalents.

## Claim lock

HIGH:
- The show is modular and cue-driven; `爆仗` repeatedly marks transitions.
- Military display, theatrical masking, acrobatics, comedy and mounted sport coexist in one court spectacle program.
- Meng preserves both genre labels and performer names.
- Female riders are explicitly described as dressed like men and following male-style ritual movement (`如男子儀`).

Editorial proposition: **THE PROGRAM.**

Guardrail: avoid `circus` as a totalizing label and avoid treating every act as military training.

# Entry 59 — 池苑內縱人關撲遊戲

Secure units:

> 池苑內除酒家藝人占外，多以綵幕繳絡，鋪設珍玉奇玩、疋帛、動使、茶酒器物關撲。

> 有以一笏撲三十笏者，以至車馬、地宅、歌姬、舞女，皆約以價而撲之。

> 出九、和合，有名者任大頭、快活三之類，餘亦不數。

> 宣政間亦有假賃大小船子，許士庶遊賞，其價有差。

## Lexical lock

- **`出九、和合`** — HIGH. Tang/Song legal material distinguishes `出九` and `和合` as gambling-related roles; 《能改齋漫錄》 also preserves `出九入十` in gaming context. Iriya Yoshitaka explicitly rejected the old claim that `出九和合` was corrupt, citing legal evidence. Punctuate as two roles: `出九、和合`.
- Functional translation should remain modest: professional operators/providers and people who gathered or arranged players. Avoid direct modern equivalences such as casino dealer, bookmaker or croupier.
- **`任大頭、快活三`** — MEDIUM-HIGH segmentation as names/nicknames of well-known practitioners attached to those roles. Biographical identities remain unknown. The separate use of `快活三` in later theatrical/music contexts does not redefine this person here.

## Claim lock

HIGH:
- `關撲` operates inside the opened imperial-garden environment.
- Meng lists an unusually broad range of goods/stakes and explicitly says values were agreed before play.
- Food stalls and boat rental are integral to the pool-side leisure economy.
- This should link internally to Entry 44 while preserving the different administrative setting.

Editorial proposition: **THE PLAY MARKET.**

# Comparative visual lock — 《金明池爭標圖》

The surviving painting known as 《金明池爭標圖》 is useful for Entries 53–54 only as **COMPARATIVE VISUAL EVIDENCE**.

Safe treatment:

> 《金明池爭標圖》頁，張擇端（款）／傳，現存作品歸屬有爭議。COMPARATIVE VISUAL EVIDENCE.

Research boundary:
- Ma Dongyao (BNU, 2020) analyzes the image through water-performance, imperial-ritual and commoner-leisure zones. That taxonomy is a modern scholarly framework, not Meng's wording.
- Palace Museum scholar Yu Hui argues that the surviving image's Zhang Zeduan attribution inscription is later and questions attribution to Zhang, while discussing the lost `西湖爭標圖` tradition.
- Never use this painting as sole proof of exact Jinming architecture, direction or dimensions.
