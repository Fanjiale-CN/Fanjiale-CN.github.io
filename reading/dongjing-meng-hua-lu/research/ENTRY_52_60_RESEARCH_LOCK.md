## Entry 52 — 清明節

### Working text lock

> 清明節，尋常京師以冬至後一百五日為大寒食。前一日謂之「炊熟」，用麵造棗䭅飛燕，柳條串之，插於門楣，謂之「子推燕」。子女及笄者，多以是日上頭。寒食第三節，即清明日矣。凡新墳皆用此日拜掃。都城人出郊。禁中前半月發宮人車馬朝陵，宗室南班近親，亦分遣詣諸陵墳享祀。從人皆紫衫、白絹三角子、青行纏，皆係官給。節日亦禁中出車馬，詣奉先寺道者院祀諸宮人墳，莫非金裝紺幰、錦額珠簾、繡扇雙遮、紗籠前導。士庶闐塞諸門，紙馬鋪皆於當街用紙衮疊成樓閣之狀。四野如市，往往就芳樹之下，或園囿之間，羅列盃盤，互相勸酬。都城之歌兒舞女，遍滿園亭。抵暮而歸，各攜棗䭅、炊餅、黃胖、掉刀、名花異果、山亭戲具、鴨卵雞雛，謂之「門外土儀」。轎子即以楊柳雜花裝簇頂上，四垂遮映。自此三日皆出城上墳，但一百五日最盛。節日坊市賣稠餳、麥糕、乳酪、乳餅之類。緩入都門，斜陽御柳；醉歸院落，明月梨花。諸軍禁衛各成隊伍，跨馬作樂四出，謂之「𣘚腳」。其旗旌鮮明，軍容雄壯，人馬精銳，又別為一景也。

### Collation / apparatus

- **`為大寒食`**: Siku-family text transmits this continuously. Gutenberg / some digital editions punctuate `為大。寒食前一日…`. A later quotation in 《歲時廣記》 explicitly cites 《東京夢華錄》 as `京師以冬至後一百五日為大寒食`, strongly supporting `大寒食` as the phrase. Working punctuation therefore uses `為大寒食。前一日…`.
- **`棗䭅飛燕`**: Siku has `棗䭅飛燕`; Gutenberg drops `䭅`. Restore the Siku character in the working text; record Gutenberg omission.
- **`紺幰`**: Siku has `紺幰`; Gutenberg digital text can appear as `紺闤`. Restore `幰` in the working text.
- **`斜陽御柳` ↔ `斜楊御栁`**: Siku transmits `斜楊`, Gutenberg `斜陽`. Contextual parallel `斜陽御柳；醉歸院落，明月梨花` strongly favors the Gutenberg reading for meaning, while the substantive variant stays visible in apparatus.
- **`𣘚腳` ↔ `摔腳`**: Siku-family witness contains the rare form `𣘚腳`; edited digital texts often give `摔腳`. The dictionary meaning of isolated `𣘚` does not securely explain the phrase. **TEXT HIGH / MEANING LOW.** Do not silently normalize to a modern performance label.

### Claim lock

HIGH:
- Qingming produces a mass movement from city to suburb / graves / gardens; `士庶闐塞諸門` and `四野如市` are Meng's own crowd-language.
- Mourning, family rites, court tomb visitation, food, entertainment, flowers, market goods and return traffic occupy the same festival field.
- `門外土儀` is Meng's label for goods brought back from outside the gates.
- Decorated sedan chairs with willow and mixed flowers are explicitly described.

CAUTION:
- `四野如市`, `歌兒舞女遍滿園亭` are Meng's vivid scale claims, not statistical counts.
- Avoid reconstructing `黃胖`, `掉刀`, `山亭戲具` beyond what the lexical evidence supports.
- `𣘚腳` stays untranslated or cautiously transliterated pending stronger lexical evidence.

Editorial proposition: **The city goes out through its gates.** Qingming is a movement system linking grave visit, suburban market, leisure and return, with mourning and spring sociability sharing the same outward flow.

## Entry 53 — 三月一日開金明池瓊林苑

The entry opens the Jinming Pool and Qionglin Garden on lunar 3/1. Meng describes access, dimensions, the Linshui Hall, Immortal Bridge, five central halls, Baojin Tower, temporary viewing structures, food shops, performance areas, trade / pawn activity, fishing, boat storage and mounted imperial guards.

Secure key clauses:

> 三月一日，州西順天門外開金明池、瓊林苑。每日教習車駕上池儀範，雖禁從，士庶許縱賞，御史臺有榜不得彈劾。

> 殿上下回廊皆關撲錢物、飲食、伎藝人作場、勾肆，羅列左右。

> 池之東岸臨水近牆皆垂楊，兩邊皆綵棚幕次，臨水假賃觀看爭標。

> 其池之西岸亦無屋宇，但垂楊蘸水，煙草鋪堤，遊人稀少，多垂釣之士。

> 車駕臨幸往往取二十日。

### Collation / apparatus

- **`西北臨水殿` ↔ edited `面北臨水殿`**: Kanripo/Siku and Gutenberg transmit `西北臨水殿`; some edited texts normalize to `面北`. Keep `西北` as transmitted working text. **P1 IMAGE / EDITION COLLATION** before making a directional reconstruction.
- **`只至閑池` ↔ `只至閉池`**: Siku-family witness has `閑池`; Gutenberg/edited versions can show `閉池`. The later Entry 60 clause `四月八日閉池` makes `閉池` semantically attractive, but this remains a substantive variant. No silent correction.
- Measurements (`周圍約九里三十步`, `直徑七里許`, etc.) remain Meng's reported measurements. Avoid converting them into a precise GIS footprint without an explicit uncertainty model.

### Claim lock

HIGH:
- The opened pool/garden combines imperial infrastructure with public viewing, market activity, performance and rental viewing space.
- East and west banks are described differently: east bank dense with viewing structures / shops; west bank quieter and associated with fishing.
- Public access is selective and spatially structured.

CAUTION:
- `士庶許縱賞` supports public visitation; it does not imply unrestricted access to every court space.
- `車駕臨幸往往取二十日` means Meng says the imperial visit often chose the twentieth day; avoid turning this into an inflexible annual calendar rule.

Editorial proposition: **An imperial landscape opens into a temporary city.** Entry 53 can establish the site plan used by Entries 54–60.

## Entry 54 — 駕幸臨水殿觀爭標錫宴

### Sequence lock

This is one of the strongest procedural texts in the book. Preserve sequence:

1. Emperor at Linshui Hall; banquet for officials.
2. Water-stage / guard display.
3. Four colored boats carrying military entertainments.
4. Two music boats.
5. Water-puppet boat: fisherman, child rower, live fish, ball play / dance by wooden figures.
6. Two painted boats with swings → `水鞦韆` dive.
7. Performance boats withdraw.
8. 20 small dragon boats with red-clad soldiers.
9. 10 tiger-head boats.
10. 2 flying-fish boats.
11. 2 loach boats, single-person dugouts.
12. Small boats pull the great dragon boat from the `奧屋`.
13. Red-flag signal system controls formations.
14. `旋羅` → `海眼` → `交頭`.
15. Final alignment before the halls.
16. `標竿` set in the water.
17. Flag signal → simultaneous advance → winner takes the marker → acclaim / bowing.
18. Three contests; boats return the great dragon boat to storage.

### Claim lock

HIGH:
- The performance is explicitly commanded by visual flag signals.
- The boats form named patterns before the final contest.
- The event blends puppetry, acrobatics, music, military personnel, civilian participants and the imperial audience.
- This is lunar third-month Jinming Pool spectacle in Meng's sequence.

P0 / unresolved:
- **`百姓卸在行人`**: exact social/occupational meaning is disputed in later commentary. Some scholarship reads `卸在行` as an unloading/loading occupational group; other annotation offers different readings. Keep source text and mark **TEXT HIGH / MEANING LOW**. Do not label confidently as dockworkers in the main narrative.
- Exact technical mechanisms of water puppets and swing apparatus are unspecified. Avoid mechanical reconstruction language stronger than the text.

Editorial proposition: **The pool behaves like a stage controlled by signals.** The best explanatory device is choreography / cue sequence, with `旗招之` as the recurring control event.

Important comparison limit: do not collapse this into modern Duanwu dragon-boat festival. Meng places this Jinming Pool spectacle in the third-month imperial outing complex.

## Comparative image lock — 《金明池爭標圖》

A surviving painting commonly known as 《金明池爭標圖》 is highly relevant to Entries 53–54 as **COMPARATIVE VISUAL EVIDENCE**.

Use attribution carefully:
- Modern scholarship commonly labels it **張擇端（款）** / attributed to Zhang Zeduan.
- Ma Dongyao (BNU, 2020) analyzes it through three interacting zones: water-performance space, imperial-ritual space, and commoner-leisure space. This is a useful secondary framework, not Meng's own taxonomy.
- Palace Museum scholar Yu Hui has challenged direct attribution of the surviving Jinmingchi painting to Zhang Zeduan while reconstructing the lost `西湖爭標圖` tradition. The website must not caption the surviving image simply as an uncontested Zhang Zeduan original.

Safe label if used:

> 《金明池爭標圖》頁，張擇端（款）／傳，現存作品歸屬有爭議。COMPARATIVE VISUAL EVIDENCE.

Do not use the painting as proof for exact architecture where textual variants remain unresolved (`西北臨水殿` / `面北臨水殿`, etc.).
