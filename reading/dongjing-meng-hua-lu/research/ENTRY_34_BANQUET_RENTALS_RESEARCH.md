Public working title:

**THE HOST ONLY PAYS**

Core proposition:

> **A KAIFENG BANQUET IS A CONTRACTED PRODUCT: FOUR SPECIALIZED BUREAUS SUPPLY IT, TERRITORIAL CREWS DELIVER IT, RATE TABLES BOUND IT, AND THE HOST'S ENTIRE JOB IS TO PAY.**

Meng describes the event-hire system in two moves. First, the division of labor: `茶酒司` rents the furniture, vessels and wine gear; `廚司` supplies food and drink; `白席人` run the floor — trays, invitations, seating, attendance, singing and toasting. Together they are the `四司人`. Second, the contract: crews have fixed territories (`地分`), established rate tables (`則例`), and they dare not overcharge (`不敢過越取錢`). However many hundreds of items a banquet involves, the hall ends up orderly — and the host's only task is money (`主人只出錢而已`).

The page is a **service contract**: the four-bureau division of labor on one side, the pricing and delivery clauses on the other.

## Working text

### 34A / THE FOUR BUREAUS

> 凡民間吉凶筵會，椅桌陳設，器皿合盤，酒檐動使之類，自有茶酒司管賃。吃食下酒，自有廚司。以至托盤、下請書、安排坐次、尊前執事、歌說勸酒，謂之「白席人」。總謂之「四司人」。

### 34B / THE CONTRACT

> 欲就園館亭榭寺院遊賞命客之類，舉意便辦，亦各有地分，承攬排備，自有則例，亦不敢過越取錢。雖百十分，廳館整肅，主人只出錢而已，不用費力。

## Secure source-derived structure

- Scope: `凡民間吉凶筵會` — ordinary families' banquets, both auspicious and funerary; the commercial system serves private households, not the court.
- `茶酒司` — rents the furniture, vessels, trays and wine gear (`椅桌陳設，器皿合盤，酒檐動使之類`).
- `廚司` — supplies food and drink (`吃食下酒`).
- `白席人` — the floor crew: carrying trays, delivering invitations, arranging seating, attending the honored place, singing and urging wine.
- `總謂之「四司人」` — collectively called the Four Bureaus' people. The text names three functions explicitly; the fourth member of the set is not identified in this entry.
- Contract terms: `舉意便辦` (on demand), `各有地分` (each crew has its territory), `承攬排備` (they contract and prepare), `自有則例` (established rate tables), `亦不敢過越取錢` (they dare not overcharge).
- Closing claim: however many hundreds of items (`雖百十分`), the hall is orderly, and the host's only task is to pay (`主人只出錢而已，不用費力`).

## Glossary (page scope)

- `吉凶筵會` — auspicious and funerary banquets.
- `茶酒司` / `廚司` / `白席人` — the three named specializations inside the `四司人` crew.
- `四司人` — the collective name; the page notes the unnamed fourth member instead of supplying one.
- `酒檐動使` — wine-load equipment / movable service gear.
- `地分` — territory / beat.
- `則例` — established rate tables.
- `不敢過越取錢` — the overcharging prohibition, quoted in the page's contract panel.

## Collation / textual uncertainty

- Siku orthographic variants: `㑹` for `會` (`筵㑹假賃`, `羅立㑹聚`), `㕔` for `廳`, `卓` for `桌` (`椅卓`), `厨` for `廚`, `巳` for `已`.
- `酒檐` — Siku and both modern transcriptions agree; kept as transmitted.

## Unresolved

- The identity of the fourth bureau in `四司人`. The text supplies three names; the page preserves the count without inventing the fourth.
- Whether `地分` implies licensed territories or customary turf. The text says `各有地分`; the page translates the phrase and resists a stronger institutional claim.
