### Spatial consequence

Because the transmitted witnesses do not support one uncontested sequence of “second gate → third gate,” the public visual system uses the label:

**MIDDLE GATE ZONE**

The interface is a text-driven relative threshold sequence, not an architectural survey.

No invented measurements, exact coordinates or precise reconstructed temple plan.

## Secure source-derived structure

The common text and project collation securely support this high-level sequence:

1. The temple opens for public trading five times each month.
2. At / around the great outer gate: birds, cats, dogs and unusual animals.
3. In the middle gate zone / court: practical and household goods, riding equipment, weapons, seasonal foods and cured foods.
4. Near the Buddhist hall: named specialty sellers associated with preserves, brushes and ink.
5. Along the two corridors: embroidery, clothing accessories, flowers, jewelry / head ornaments, decorative headwear and thread.
6. Before Zisheng Gate behind the hall: books, curios, paintings, regional products and aromatics / medicines associated with officials returning from posts.
7. In the rear corridor: divination and portrait / physiognomic services.
8. The same complex contains bronze arhats, a Buddha-tooth relic, towers, monastic courts, ritual banquets, famous calligraphy / painting and offering / performance spaces.

## Page text selections

These are selected text blocks for the Reading page, not a claim to replace a full diplomatic transcription.

### 18A / OPENING + MARKET THRESHOLDS

> 相國寺每月五次開放萬姓交易，大三門上皆是飛禽貓犬之類，珍禽奇獸，無所不有。第三門皆動用什物，庭中設彩幕露屋義鋪，賣蒲合、簟席、屏幃、洗漱、鞍轡、弓劍、時果、臘脯之類。近佛殿，孟家道冠王道人蜜煎、趙文秀筆及潘谷墨占定。

Working translation:

Xiangguo Temple opens five times each month for public trading. Around the great gate are birds, cats, dogs and unusual animals. In the middle gate zone and court are practical goods: mats and screens, washing articles, saddles and bridles, bows and swords, seasonal fruit and cured foods. Near the Buddhist hall, Meng-family / Wang Daoren preserves, Zhao Wenxiu brushes and Pan Gu ink appear as named specialties.

Translation note: the public page should not force `第三門` into a measured architectural route. The interface label remains **MIDDLE GATE ZONE**.

### 18B / CORRIDORS + REAR GATE

> 兩廊，皆諸寺師姑賣繡作、領抹、花朵、珠翠頭面、生色銷金花樣襆頭帽子、特髻冠子、絛線之類。殿後資聖門前，皆書籍玩好圖畫及諸路罷任官員土物香藥之類。後廊皆日者貨術、傳神之類。

Working translation:

Along the two corridors, religious women sell embroidery, clothing accessories, flowers, jewelry and head ornaments, decorative headwear and thread. Before Zisheng Gate behind the hall are books, curios, paintings, regional products and aromatics or medicines associated with officials returning from posts. The rear corridor includes divination and portrait-like services.

Translation note: `傳神` is kept cautious in public copy; do not overspecify the service beyond the transmitted wording without specialist support.

### 18C / SACRED + RITUAL CORE

> 寺三門閣上並資聖門，各有金銅鑄羅漢五百尊、佛牙等，凡有齋供，皆取旨方開三門。左右有兩瓶琉璃塔，寺內有智海、惠林、寶梵、河沙東西塔院，乃出角院舍，各有住持僧官，每遇齋會，凡飲食茶果，動使器皿，雖三五百分，莫不咄嗟而辦。大殿兩廊，皆國朝名公筆跡。殿庭供獻樂部馬隊之類。大殿朵廊，皆壁隱樓殿人物，莫非精妙。

Working translation:

The market exists inside a functioning religious complex. Meng records bronze arhats and a Buddha-tooth relic, towers and monastic courts, ritual gatherings with food and vessels, works associated with notable calligraphers or painters, and offering / performance activity in the temple court.

Translation note: disputed wall-painting phrases are not used as the page’s core factual claim until the exact wording is collated again.

## Visual system lock

The flagship interaction is an eight-stage threshold sequence:

`OPENING → GREAT GATE → MIDDLE GATE ZONE → HALL EDGE → TWO CORRIDORS → ZISHENG GATE → REAR CORRIDOR → RITUAL CORE`

Each stage must expose both its commercial inventory and its relation to the sacred complex where supported.

The page is not an exact map. It is a **text-driven spatial section**.

A separate rhythm marker states **FIVE OPENINGS / MONTH** without inventing dates or a continuous daily market.

## Interaction / accessibility lock

- Core content exists in HTML before JavaScript.
- JavaScript progressively enhances the threshold sequence.
- Arrow Left / Right / Up / Down moves between stages; Home / End jump to first / last.
- Animation uses one short opacity / transform transition, target duration 220ms.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` removes the transition.
- On narrow screens all information remains readable without requiring the interactive state.
