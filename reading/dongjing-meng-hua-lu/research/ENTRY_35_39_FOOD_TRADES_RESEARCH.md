## Working text — Entry 35 會仙酒樓

### 35A / THE SHOP

> 如州東仁和店、新門裏會仙樓正店，常有百十分廳館，動使各各足備，不尚少闕一件。

### 35B / THE SILVER STANDARD

> 大抵都人風俗奢侈，度量稍寬，凡酒店中不問何人，止兩人對坐飲酒，亦須用注碗一副，盤盞兩副，果菜碟各五片，水菜碗三五隻，即銀近百兩矣。雖一人獨飲，碗遂亦用銀盂之類。其果子菜蔬，無非精潔。若別要下酒，即使人外買軟羊、龜背、大小骨、諸色包子、玉板鮓、生削巴子、瓜薑之類。

Secure structure: the section opens with `如` — it instantiates the `正店` standard Entry 13 surveyed. `動使` (movable service gear) is always complete (`各各足備，不尚少闕一件`). The tableware minimum applies regardless of person (`不問何人`) or party size (`止兩人對坐`, `雖一人獨飲`). The set: one pour-bowl set, two dish-and-cup sets, five fruit-and-dish plates each, three-to-five water dishes — pricing out near a hundred taels of silver (`即銀近百兩矣`). Cooked accompaniments are bought in from outside (`即使人外買…`): soft lamb, turtle-back cakes, large and small bones, buns, jade-board fish, raw sliced pork, pickled ginger.

## Working text — Entry 36 食店

### 36A / THE TAXONOMY

> 大凡食店，大者謂之「分茶」，則有頭羹、石髓羹、白肉、胡餅、軟羊、大小骨角、炙犒腰子、石肚羹、入爐羊罨、生軟羊麵、桐皮麵、薑潑刀、回刀、冷淘、棋子、寄爐麵飯之類。吃全茶，饒齏頭羹。更有川飯店，則有插肉麵、大燠麵、大小抹肉淘、煎燠肉、雜煎事件、生熟燒飯。更有南食店：魚兜子、桐皮熟膾麵、煎魚飯。

### 36B / THE HOUSE AND THE RELAY

> 又有瓠羹店，門前以枋木及花樣礬結縛如山棚，上掛成邊豬羊，相間三二十邊。近裏門面窗戶，皆朱綠裝飾，謂之「歡門」。每店各有廳院東西廊稱呼坐次。客坐，則一人執箸紙，遍問坐客。都人侈縱，百端呼索，或熱或冷，或溫或整，或絕冷、精澆、膘澆之類，人人索喚不同。行菜得之，近局次立，從頭唱念，報與局內。當局者謂之「鐺頭」，又曰「著案」訖。

### 36C / THE DELIVERY

> 須臾，行菜者左手杈三碗、右臂自手至肩馱疊約二十碗，散下盡合各人呼索，不容差錯。一有差錯，坐客白之主人，必加叱罵，或罰工價，甚者逐之。吾輩入店，則用一等琉璃淺稜碗，謂之「碧碗」，亦謂之「造羹」，菜蔬精細，謂之「造齏」，每碗十文。麵與肉相停，謂之「合羹」；又有「單羹」，乃半箇也。舊只用匙，今皆用箸矣。更有插肉、撥刀、炒羊、細物料、棋子、餛飩店。及有素分茶，如寺院齋食也。又有菜麵、蝴蝶齏胳，及賣隨飯、荷包、白飯、旋切細料餶飿兒、瓜齏、蘿蔔之類。

Secure structure: four shop classes (`分茶` houses, `川飯店` Sichuan houses, `南食店` southern-food houses, `瓠羹店` gourd-soup houses) each with a fixed dish list. The gourd-soup house carries the physical theatre: timber-and-patterned alum frames bound like a mountain gate (`山棚`), whole pigs and sheep hung in rows of twenty to thirty (`成邊豬羊，相間三二十邊`), the red-and-green `歡門`. Service is a relay: one checker with the order pad (`執箸紙`) queries every diner; diners call across temperature and cut (`或熱或冷…人人索喚不同`); the runner chants the order back (`從頭唱念`) to the kitchen post (`報與局內`), where the `鐺頭` / `著案` receives it; minutes later the runner carries three bowls on the left hand and some twenty stacked up the right arm and lands every order without error — mistakes cost a scolding, a wage fine, or the job. The price floor: glass-rimmed `碧碗` stew at ten cash a bowl; `合羹` is noodles and meat half-and-half, `單羹` half of that. The utensil history: once only spoons, now chopsticks (`舊只用匙，今皆用箸矣`).

## Working text — Entry 37 肉行

### 37A / THE STALL

> 坊巷橋市，皆有肉案，列三五人操刀，生熟肉從便索喚，闊切、片批、細抹、頓刀之類。

### 37B / THE SHIFT AND THE MONEY

> 至晚即有燠爆熟食上市。凡買物不上數錢得者是數。

Secure structure: meat cases (`肉案`) in every lane and bridge market, three to five knife-men each, raw and cooked meat cut to order; four cutting formats (`闊切` wide, `片批` sliced, `細抹` fine-shaved, `頓刀` chopped); an evening shift of roasted and flash-fried cooked food (`燠爆熟食上市`); and small-change retail — a few cash buys an exact amount (`不上數錢得者是數`).

## Working text — Entry 38 餅店

### 38A / TWO SHOP CLASSES

> 凡餅店有油餅店，有胡餅店。若油餅店，即賣蒸餅、糖餅，裝合引盤之類。

### 38B / THE PRODUCTION LINE

> 胡餅店即賣門油、菊花、寬焦、側厚、油碢、髓餅、新樣滿麻。每案用三五人捍劑卓花入爐。自五更卓案之聲遠近相聞。唯武成王廟前海州張家、皇建院前鄭家最盛，每家有五十餘爐。

Secure structure: two classes (oil-bing shops: steamed and sugar bings, boxed sets and serving trays; hu-bing shops: seven named flatbreads — 門油, 菊花, 寬焦, 側厚, 油碢, 髓餅, 新樣滿麻). Production cells of three to five workers per bench (`每案三五人`): knead (`捍劑`), shape-stamp (`卓花`), fire (`入爐`). At the fifth watch the sound of benches carries for miles (`自五更卓案之聲遠近相聞`). Two market leaders: Hai-state Zhang's before the Wucheng King temple and Zheng's before Huangjian Yuan, each with fifty-plus ovens (`每家有五十餘爐`).

## Working text — Entry 39 魚行

### 39A / THE MORNING INTAKE

> 賣生魚則用淺抱桶，以柳葉間串清水中浸，或循街出賣，每日早惟新鄭門、西水門、萬勝門，如此生魚有數千簷入門。

### 39B / THE WINTER CAR-FISH

> 冬月即黃河諸遠處客魚來，謂之「車魚」，每斤不上一百文。

Secure structure: live fish retailed from shallow carry-buckets (`淺抱桶`), willow leaves strung through clear water (`以柳葉間串清水中浸`), hawked street by street; each dawn three gates — Xinzheng, Xishui, Wansheng — admit thousands of shoulder-loads (`數千簷入門`); in winter the Yellow River's distant "cart fish" (`車魚`) arrive, under a hundred cash per catty.

## Chronology / evidence status

- All five entries are standing descriptions of trades; none carries a date.
- Memory horizon (site-established): Meng in the capital from 1103; capital lost 1127; book completed 1147.
- Shop names, dish lists, service protocol, production figures, gate names and prices: TEXT / CONFIRMED across witnesses.
- `即銀近百兩` is the entry's own estimate (`近`), not a rate schedule; the page must not convert it to a modern price.
- `炙犒腰子`: the炙 is supported by 殆知閣 and (corruptly) by the Siku transcription; Gutenberg omits it. Included as the majority reading; flagged on the page.

## Collation / textual uncertainty

- 35 `果菜碟各五片`: Gutenberg and 殆知閣 print 片; the Siku transcription prints 果菜楪各五斤. The page keeps 片 and records 斤.
- 35 `新門裏`: Siku 裏, Gutenberg 裡. The page keeps 裏 (standard Hant).
- 36 `花樣礬`: Gutenberg prints 礬; the Siku transcription prints a divergent glyph; 殆知閣 prints 启 — three witnesses, three renderings of one character. The page keeps the Gutenberg form and leaves the crux open.
- 36 `蝴蝶齏胳`: Gutenberg prints 胳; the Siku transcription prints 肐 plus a character its digital text leaves entity-encoded; 殆知閣 likewise ends in an unidentified glyph. The exact food name is transmission-unstable; the page keeps the Gutenberg form and says so.
- 36 `餶飿兒`: Gutenberg OCR gap; Siku prints 餶飿兒; 殆知閣 agrees in simplified form. Gap filled from Siku.
- 36 `膘澆` ↔ Siku 臕澆; `須臾` ↔ Siku 須㬰; `著案` ↔ Siku 着案; `歡門` ↔ Siku 驩門 — orthographic variants, kept in the trail.
- 38 `寬焦`: Gutenberg drops 寬 (`菊花、焦、側厚`); Siku and 殆知閣 print 寬焦. Gap filled from Siku.
- 38 `油碢`: Gutenberg OCR gap; Siku prints 油碢; 殆知閣 prints 油栗. The page follows the Siku witness for the filled gap and records the divergence.
- 38 `卓花 / 卓案`: both primary witnesses print 卓 here; the working text keeps 卓 and notes the `卓 ↔ 桌` family already collated in Entry 34.
- 39 `數千簷`: Gutenberg 簷, Siku 檐. The site's Entry 31 collation kept 檐 for the vehicle term; this page keeps the Gutenberg form 簷 for the load word and records both.
- 35 heading vs text: the section heading names 會仙酒樓; the opening sentence names 會仙樓正店 (with 仁和店). Both forms are transmitted; the page uses the heading as title and the text as text.
