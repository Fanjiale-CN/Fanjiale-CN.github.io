## Working primary text

上清宮，在新宋門裏街北，以西茆山下院。醴泉觀，在東水門裏。觀音院，在舊宋門後太廟南門。景德寺，在上清宮背，寺前有桃花洞，皆妓館。開寶寺，在舊封丘門外斜街子，內有二十四院，惟仁王院最盛。天清寺，在州北清暉橋。興德院，在金水門外。長生宮，在鹿家巷。顯寧寺，在炭場巷。北婆臺寺，在陳州門裏。兜率寺，在紅門道地。踴佛寺，在州西草場巷街。南十方靜因院，在州西油醋巷。浴室院，在第三條甜水巷。福田院，在舊曹門外。報恩寺，在卸鹽巷。太和宮女道士，在州西洪橋子大街。洞元觀女道士，在班樓北。瑤華宮，在金水門外。萬壽觀，在舊酸棗門外十王宮前。

## Witness comparison used for the page

Common Wikisource and the Siku transcription agree on the overall directory and location sequence. Two visible differences are retained:

| Common electronic text | Siku | Page decision |
|---|---|---|
| 仁王院 | 仁玉院 | retain 仁王院 in the working text; expose Siku variant |
| 洞元觀又道士 | 洞元觀女道士 | use 女道士 as working lock; expose common variant |

The second decision is supported by the parallel construction immediately before it: 太和宮女道士. It remains a working collation decision, not a claim that all witnesses are uniform.

## Editorial reading

Entry 20 is valuable as religious geography. It shifts away from the inside/outside sequence of Entries 18–19 and names institutions across the capital through gates, bridges, lanes and street anchors.

Public framing:
**A capital overlaid by a dispersed network of named religious institutions.**

Avoid:
- precise GIS reconstruction;
- invented coordinates;
- decorative “ancient temple map” treatment;
- assigning Buddhist / Daoist affiliation where the text does not securely permit it.
