## Editorial role

Entry 32 is Volume IV / 5 OF 12. It opens the volume's rental block after the court entries.

Batch arc:

`COURT IN MOTION → THE RENTAL CITY`

Public working title:

**DEATH HAS A PRICE LIST**

Core proposition:

> **THE CITY RENTS THE TWO THINGS NO ONE CAN REFUSE: FUNERALS AND DISTANCE.**

Meng compresses the rental economy into two price signals. For funerals, the `凶肆` (funeral establishments) keep fixed procedures and fixed prices for every item of the cortege — `方相`, `車輿`, `結絡`, `綵帛`. For daily mobility, a hired saddled horse can be found by the lane, bridge or market, and it never costs more than one hundred cash (`不過百錢`).

The page is a **two-panel price board**: death logistics with fixed tariffs, and mobility with a stated ceiling.

## Working text

### 32A / FUNERAL TARIFF + HIRED HORSE

> 若凶事出殯，自上而下，凶肆各有體例。如方相、車輿、結絡、綵帛，皆有定價，不須勞力。尋常出街市幹事，稍似路遠倦行，逐坊巷橋市，自有假賃鞍馬者，不過百錢。

## Secure source-derived structure

- `凶事出殯` — the entry is about funerals and cortege processions, the `凶` (inauspicious) side of the same commercial city Entry 34 will describe on the `吉` (auspicious) side.
- `自上而下` — "from the top down": read on the page as spanning status levels from grand to humble; the phrase itself is terse and the page keeps the reading marked.
- `凶肆` — funeral establishments, each with its own established procedures (`各有體例`).
- The four named cortege items are `方相` (the exorcist figure led at the head of the procession), `車輿` (funeral vehicles), `結絡` (decorative netting / streamers) and `綵帛` (colored silks).
- `皆有定價，不須勞力` — everything carries a fixed price; the client does not have to labor over arrangements.
- `假賃鞍馬者，不過百錢` — hired saddled horses exist along lanes, bridges and markets; the ceiling is one hundred cash. The page cross-references Entry 23 / 《都市錢陌》 for what a "hundred" meant in Kaifeng counting conventions; it does not convert to modern money.

## Glossary (page scope)

- `凶肆` — funeral establishments.
- `方相` — the exorcist figure that led funeral processions; a tradition traced on the page no further than its processional role.
- `車輿` — funeral vehicles.
- `結絡` — decorative netting / streamers on the cortege (page reading; annotator glosses not asserted).
- `綵帛` — colored silks for funeral display.
- `鞍馬` — saddled horse for hire.
- `百錢` — one hundred cash, a ceiling price, read against the 錢陌 conventions of Entry 23.
