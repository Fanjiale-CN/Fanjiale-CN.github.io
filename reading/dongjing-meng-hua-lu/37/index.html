肉行

Cut to order.

The meat trade is the shortest section in the volume and one of the most exact. Every lane and bridge market kept open cases, three to five knife-men to a stall, raw and cooked sold to call — and the whole trade compressed itself into four cutting formats. The knife, not the cut of the animal, decided what you bought.

1. The case, the crew, the call.

PRIMARY TEXT37A / THE STALL
坊巷橋市，皆有肉案，列三五人操刀，生熟肉從便索喚，闊切、片批、細抹、頓刀之類。
WORKING TRANSLATION

In the lanes and at the bridge markets there were meat cases everywhere, three to five men to a bench handling the knife; raw meat and cooked were called for as the buyer pleased — wide cuts, slice-shaves, fine shaves, heavy chops, and the like.

GALOK READING

Distribution first: 皆有 — the cases were everywhere, not zoned to a market district. Crew sizes are given as a range, which makes the stall a small firm, not a solo trade. And the product is defined twice over: by state (raw or cooked) and by cut. The buyer's verb — 索喚, to call and summon — recurs across this volume's trades, from banquet bureaus to hiring pools.

2. The evening shift, and the arithmetic of small change.

PRIMARY TEXT37B / THE SHIFT AND THE MONEY
至晚即有燠爆熟食上市。凡買物不上數錢得者是數。
WORKING TRANSLATION

Come evening, roasted and flash-fried cooked foods came to market. Whatever you bought for no more than a few cash, you got in just that amount.

GALOK READING

The second sentence is the trade's retail claim. 不上數錢得者是數 — a few cash buys an exact quantity — describes a market fluent in small change: meat cut to the coin, not the coin cut to the meat. Read together with the four formats, the case offered precision at both ends: the knife defined the shape, the cash defined the weight. The page resists the stronger claim (standard weights, licensing) because the text makes none.

02 / THE SHORTEST SECTION IN THE VOLUMEThe knife is the menu.

Entry 37 spends three sentences on the meat trade and leaves nothing out: where the cases stood, how many men worked them, what the knife could do, when the cooked shift came on, and what a coin bought. Most sections in this volume earn their length; this one earns its brevity — and the page keeps it.
