The city eats late.

Meng returns to Mahang Street and reveals an urban routine built around bought prepared food, long retail hours and people coming home deep into the night. Entry 21 is the first full Song-book aesthetics pilot in Galok Reading.

The market closes. Then it starts again.

The source gives watches, not modern clock times. The sequence preserves that historical time language and treats the interval as a reset, not a precise number of minutes.

04 / VISUAL WITNESS

Read the street beside a scroll — carefully.

Prepared food and extended retail hours make the chapter a small operating diagram of urban time: commercial households buy from the street; night trade closes, restarts and sometimes barely stops.← VOLUME III

Entry 20 spread religious institutions across the city. Entry 21 returns to one of its most active commercial corridors and changes the question again: how does ordinary urban life keep feeding itself after dark?

1. The street provides food as a household service.

PRIMARY TEXT21A / STREET + PREPARED FOOD
馬行北去，舊封丘門外祅廟斜街、州北瓦子，新封丘門大街兩邊民戶鋪席外，餘諸班直軍營相對，至門約十里餘。其餘坊巷院落，縱橫萬數，莫知紀極。處處擁門，各有茶坊酒店，勾肆飲食。市井經紀之家，往往只於市店旋置飲食，不置家蔬。北食則礬樓前李四家、段家熝物、石逢巴子；南食則寺橋金家、九曲子周家，最為屈指。
WORKING TRANSLATION

North from Mahang Street, past the old Fengqiu Gate area and toward the new Fengqiu Gate avenue, households, shopfronts and military compounds extended through a vast web of lanes and courtyards. Tea houses, wine shops, entertainment and food appeared repeatedly. Households engaged in urban commerce often obtained food from shops as needed rather than keeping all food preparation at home. Meng then names prominent northern- and southern-style food sellers.

GALOK READING

The economic point is dependence on purchased prepared food among a named group of urban households. The passage supports a service relationship between household life and street retail. It gives no defensible citywide percentage, and the named sellers should not be turned into a modern restaurant ranking.

2. The market has a reset built into the night.

PRIMARY TEXT21B / NIGHT CLOCK
夜市直至三更盡，纔五更又復開張。如耍鬧去處，通曉不絕。
WORKING TRANSLATION

The night market continued until the third watch ended; by the fifth watch it opened again. In especially busy places, activity continued through dawn without interruption.

GALOK READING

The striking fact is the rhythm: late trading, a compressed reset, then predawn reopening. Historical watches stay as watches here. Turning them into one fixed pair of modern clock times would create precision the source does not provide.

3. Night trade reaches the edges — and survives winter weather.

PRIMARY TEXT21C / EDGES + WINTER + TEA
尋常四梢遠靜去處，夜市亦有燋酸豏、豬胰胡餅、和菜餅、貛兒、野狐肉、果木翹羹、灌腸、香糖果子之類。冬月雖大風雪陰雨，亦有夜市：𠝝子、薑豉、抹臟、紅絲、水晶膾、煎肝臟、蛤蜊、螃蟹、胡桃、澤州餳、奇豆、鵝梨、石榴、查子、榲桲、糍糕、團子、鹽豉湯之類。至三更方有提瓶賣茶者。蓋都人公私營幹，夜深方歸也。
WORKING TRANSLATION

Even in quieter outlying districts there were night-market foods of many kinds. In winter, despite strong wind, snow or rain, Meng still records night trading. Tea sellers carrying bottles remained until the third watch, which he explains by noting that people occupied with public and private business returned home late.

GALOK READING

The final sentence ties retail time to labor time. Late tea sales are presented as a response to people whose work or business keeps them out late. The market clock is therefore part of the city's service infrastructure, not merely nightlife spectacle.

EVIDENCE LIMIT / WATCH TIME KEEP THE WATCHES.

三更、五更 carry historical temporal meaning, but they should not be flattened into universal exact clock times in the hero. The page preserves sequence and relative timing while leaving conversion to a separate historical note if later research justifies one.
