DONGJING MENG HUA LU / 卷四

餅店

Fifty ovens before dawn.

The bread trade ran on two shop classes, a three-step bench line and fire — and it began before the city woke. At the fifth watch the sound of the kneading boards carried far and near, and two shops answered for fifty ovens each: Zhang's before the Wucheng King temple, Zheng's before Huangjian Yuan.

38 / 餅店

Two classes, seven breads, three bench steps, and two champions with fifty ovens each.↗ TWO SHOP CLASSES↗ TEXT DETECTIVE

1. The classes and their goods.

PRIMARY TEXT38A / TWO SHOP CLASSES
凡餅店有油餅店，有胡餅店。若油餅店，即賣蒸餅、糖餅，裝合引盤之類。
WORKING TRANSLATION

In bread shops there were oil-bing shops and there were hu-bing shops. Oil-bing shops sold steamed bings and sugar bings, boxed sets and serving trays, and the like.

GALOK READING

The trade opens with a classification, not a scene: the entry's first verb is a sorting. 油餅店 and 胡餅店 divide the market by method — steamed versus baked — and each class carries a product line as fixed as a shop sign. The oil-bing shop's list ends in packaging (裝合引盤): bread as a takeaway object, boxed and trayed.

2. The line, the sound, the champions.

PRIMARY TEXT38B / THE PRODUCTION LINE
胡餅店即賣門油、菊花、寬焦、側厚、油碢、髓餅、新樣滿麻。每案用三五人捍劑卓花入爐。自五更卓案之聲遠近相聞。唯武成王廟前海州張家、皇建院前鄭家最盛，每家有五十餘爐。
WORKING TRANSLATION

Hu-bing shops sold menyou, juhua, kuanjiao, cehou, youtuo, suibing, and the new-style manma. Each bench used three to five people to knead the dough, shape and stamp it, and fire it. From the fifth watch the sound of the benches carried, near and far answering each other. Only Hai-state Zhang's before the Wucheng King temple and Zheng's before Huangjian Yuan were most flourishing — each house kept fifty and more ovens.

GALOK READING

The sentence structure is the industry's: a product list, a crew-per-bench figure, a fixed three-verb sequence — 捍劑, 卓花, 入爐 — and then sound as scale. The fifth-watch detail converts output into something the whole city heard before it saw. The champions are named by location as much as by family, and measured in ovens — the entry's only capacity figure, and the page keeps it as transmitted: fifty and more.

02 / BREAD BEFORE DAWNThe city wakes to kneading.

Entry 38 documents industry with a number where other entries use adjectives: seven breads, three steps, five hands a bench, fifty ovens. It is also the volume's quietest technological note — a market that organized itself around fire before sunrise. The bakery is the food city's clock.
