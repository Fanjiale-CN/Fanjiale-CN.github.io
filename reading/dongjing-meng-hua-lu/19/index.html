寺東門街巷

Beyond the east gate.

Leave Xiangguo Temple and the market does not disappear. It branches: books and headwear at the gate, embroidery and southern food in side lanes, medicine and boots farther north, then guesthouses, wine, offices and three Sweetwater lanes toward the ancestral temple.

19 / 寺東門街巷

The temple market becomes street frontage, side lanes, specialist shops, hospitality and institutions. This is a sequence of addresses, not a survey plan.← VOLUME III

Entry 18 organized commerce by thresholds inside Xiangguo Temple. Entry 19 changes the grammar. The text now moves by direction words and proper names; specialization remains, but it is dispersed through an urban network rather than contained by one institution.

1. The east-gate frontage already mixes several economies.

PRIMARY TEXT19A / EAST GATE + SIDE LANES
寺東門大街，皆是襆頭、腰帶、書籍、冠朵鋪席，丁家素茶。寺南即錄事巷妓館。繡巷皆師姑繡作居住。北即小甜水巷，巷內南食店甚盛，妓館亦多。向北李慶糟薑鋪。
WORKING TRANSLATION

On the main street outside the temple's east gate were shops associated with headwear, belts, books and head ornaments, together with Ding Family's plain-tea shop. South of the temple was Lushi Lane with courtesan houses. Embroidery Lane was occupied by religious women engaged in embroidery work. To the north was Little Sweetwater Lane, where southern-style food shops were especially numerous; courtesan houses were also common. Farther north stood Li Qing's preserved-ginger shop.

Adjacency and sequence follow the text; shop counts and parcel boundaries are not reconstructed.Translation: Galok / working translation
GALOK READING

What spills from the temple is not one commodity cluster. Dress, books, tea, textile work, food and entertainment sit in close textual succession. The useful claim is mixed urban specialization, not a modern zoning analogy.

2. A medicine shop contains a second city: painted mountains.

PRIMARY TEXT19B / NORTH + EAST BRANCH
直北出景靈宮東門前。又向北曲東稅務街、高頭街、薑行後巷，乃脂皮畫曲妓館。南北講堂巷、孫殿丞藥鋪、靴店。出界北巷，巷口宋家生藥鋪，本鋪中兩壁皆李成所畫山水。
WORKING TRANSLATION

Continuing north leads toward the east gate of Jingling Palace, then the sequence bends east through Shuiwu Street, Gaotou Street and the lane behind the ginger trade. Around Jiangtang Lane the text names a medicine shop and boot shops. At the mouth of Jiebe Lane stood the Song Family crude-drug shop; Meng says that both walls inside were painted with landscapes by Li Cheng.

The difficult phrase around 脂皮畫曲 is not expanded into a confident modern place label in this draft.Translation: Galok / working translation
ART / LIMIT

Li Cheng matters here because of placement. The text locates his landscapes on a medicine shop's two walls. It does not establish an art sale, a gallery function, a commissioning history or a date.

3. Eastward, commerce and institutions interleave.

PRIMARY TEXT19C / TOWARD TAIMIAO
自景靈宮東門大街向東，街北舊乾明寺，沿火改作五寺三監。以東向南曰第三條甜水巷，以東熙熙樓客店，都下著數。以東街南高陽正店，向北入馬行。向東，街北曰車輅院，南曰第二甜水巷。以東審計院，以東桐樹子韓家，直抵太廟前門。南往觀音院，乃第一條甜水巷也。太廟北入榆林巷，通曹門大街，不能遍數也。
WORKING TRANSLATION

East along the main street from Jingling Palace's east gate, the text passes the former Qianming Temple site and its later institutional reuse; the third Sweetwater Lane; Xixi Tower guesthouse, counted among the capital's notable establishments; Gaoyang principal wine house; the carriage office; the second Sweetwater Lane; the audit office; and onward to the front of the imperial ancestral temple. South lay the first Sweetwater Lane; north of the ancestral temple, Yulin Lane connected toward Caomen Street. Meng ends by saying the places could not all be enumerated.

Three Sweetwater lanes are textual anchors; their modern geometry is not inferred here.Translation: Galok / working translation

Street names are exactly where false precision becomes dangerous.

The common electronic text and the Siku/WYG-family line diverge at several names. This REVIEW draft follows the Siku/WYG forms where CText / Gutenberg agree, while keeping the common forms visible.
