DONGJING MENG HUA LU / 卷四

雜賃

Death has a price list.

Meng compresses the rental economy into two price signals. Funeral establishments keep fixed procedures and fixed prices for every piece of the cortege. A hired horse can be found by any lane, bridge or market — and it never costs more than one hundred cash.

01 / THE MOBILITY CEILING

Distance is capped at a hundred cash.

For ordinary business across the city, when the road runs long and the legs tire, hired saddled horses wait along the lanes, bridges and markets. The entry gives one number as its ceiling. 1. Grief is itemized like inventory.

PRIMARY TEXT32A / FUNERAL TARIFF + HIRED HORSE
若凶事出殯，自上而下，凶肆各有體例。如方相、車輿、結絡、綵帛，皆有定價，不須勞力。尋常出街市幹事，稍似路遠倦行，逐坊巷橋市，自有假賃鞍馬者，不過百錢。
WORKING TRANSLATION

For funerals and burial processions — from the top of the scale down — the funeral establishments each kept their own procedures. The exorcist figure, the funeral vehicles, the netting and the colored silks all carried fixed prices; there was no need to labor over arrangements. For ordinary business in the streets, when the way was a little long and walking tired you, saddled horses for hire could be found along the lanes, bridges and markets — never costing more than a hundred cash.

GALOK READING

The entry names no rite, no family and no shop. The information Meng keeps is that prices exist, are fixed, and are not negotiated item by item. Grief becomes one more standard article of urban commerce — and the same sentence structure that prices the dead then prices the tired walker.

02 / TEXT DETECTIVE

A short entry with two open readings.

自上而下INTERPRETATION

Read here as "across status levels, from grand to humble." The alternative — "from the start to the finish of the rites" — remains compatible with the sentence and is not ruled out.

03 / PRICE BOARD COMPLETEGrief is priced. Distance is capped.

Entry 32 shows the rental economy handling the city's two non-negotiables. The funeral is itemized like inventory; the walk across town is capped like a fare. In both, the customer's effort has already been priced out of the transaction.

皆有定價，不須勞力。
