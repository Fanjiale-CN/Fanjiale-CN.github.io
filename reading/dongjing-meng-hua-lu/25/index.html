1. Patrol and observation distribute detection.

PRIMARY TEXT25A / DETECTION
每坊巷三百步許，有軍巡鋪屋一所，鋪兵五人，夜間巡警及領公事。又於高處磚砌望火樓，樓上有人卓望。下有官屋數間，屯駐軍兵百餘人，及有救火家事，謂如大小桶、洒子、麻搭、斧鋸、梯子、火杈、大索、鐵貓兒之類。
WORKING TRANSLATION

Meng says that in lanes, at intervals he describes as roughly three hundred bu, there was a military patrol post with five soldiers responsible for night patrol and official duties. High brick fire-watch towers held observers; official buildings below housed more than one hundred soldiers and firefighting equipment.

GALOK READING

Attention is distributed across street level and height. The patrol post handles local presence; the tower extends visual detection; personnel and tools are already placed below.

2. A sighting becomes a message, then a multi-body response.

PRIMARY TEXT25B / REPORT + RESPONSE
每遇有遺火去處，則有馬軍奔報。軍廂主馬步軍、殿前三衙、開封府各領軍汲水撲滅，不勞百姓。
WORKING TRANSLATION

When a fire occurred, mounted soldiers hurried to report it. Meng then names military and government bodies that led personnel to draw water and suppress the fire, adding that ordinary residents were not required for the main response labor.

GALOK READING

The sequence is unusually legible: observe, report, mobilize, suppress. Meng also identifies more than one responding authority, which makes the network organizational as well as spatial.

03 / TECHNICAL CROSS-TEXT

《武經總要》 preserves a broader Song vocabulary of fire-response tools.

The military encyclopedia 《武經總要》 was compiled in the Northern Song for warfare and city defense. Its fire-response passages name tools such as water sprinkling devices, 麻搭, hooks and 鐵貓. Several terms overlap or closely parallel Meng's urban list.

東京夢華錄洒子 · 麻搭 · 鐵貓兒

Stored below a Kaifeng fire-watch tower alongside buckets, axes, saws, ladders and rope.

武經總要柳洒子 · 麻搭 · 鐵貓

Appears within siege-defense instructions and lists of equipment kept ready on city walls.

WHAT THE OVERLAP MEANSSHARED TECHNICAL WORLD

The parallel strengthens identification of a Song fire-response toolkit. It does not make Meng's urban fire organization identical to military siege defense.

05 / HISTORICAL FRAME

Fire prevention was a real administrative problem across Song cities.

Modern historical research on Song firefighting treats watchtowers, regulations, personnel, tools and coordinated response as parts of a wider fire-safety system. Meng's Kaifeng passage is especially valuable because it compresses location, staffing, observation, communication and equipment into a single eyewitness-style urban memory.

The page keeps its strongest claims anchored to Meng and 《武經總要》. Later scholarship provides context and terminology, not replacement evidence.
