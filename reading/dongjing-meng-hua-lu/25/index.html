Local patrol, high observation, message transmission and organized response form one urban safety network.↗ ENTRY 01 / URBAN DEFENSE

1. Patrol and observation distribute detection.

PRIMARY TEXT25A / DETECTION
每坊巷三百步許，有軍巡鋪屋一所，鋪兵五人，夜間巡警收領公事。又於高處磚砌望火樓，樓上有人卓望。下有官屋數間，屯駐軍兵百餘人，及有救火家事，謂如大小桶、酒子、麻搭、斧鋸、梯子、火叉、大索、鐵貓兒之類。
WORKING TRANSLATION

Meng says that at intervals he describes as roughly three hundred bu in lanes there was a military patrol post with five soldiers, responsible for night patrol and official duties. High brick fire-watch towers held observers; official buildings below housed more than one hundred soldiers and firefighting equipment.

GALOK READING

The system distributes attention. A local patrol layer and an elevated observation layer are backed by staged manpower and tools. The historical distance remains 三百步許; no exact modern block spacing is inferred.

2. A sighting becomes a message, then a multi-body response.

PRIMARY TEXT25B / REPORT + RESPONSE
每遇有遺火去處，則有馬軍奔報。軍廂主馬步軍、殿前三衙、開封府各領軍級撲滅，不勞百姓。
WORKING TRANSLATION

When a fire occurred, mounted soldiers hurried to report it. Meng then names military and government bodies that led personnel to suppress the fire, adding that ordinary residents were not required for the main response labor.

GALOK READING

The chapter’s clearest systems insight is the chain from detection to communication to organized suppression. It can be compared structurally with an emergency-response network while keeping the Song institutions and their exact divisions distinct from modern fire services.
