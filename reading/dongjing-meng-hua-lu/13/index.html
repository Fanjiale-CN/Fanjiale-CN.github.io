13 / 酒樓

Five text units move from Rendian's interior to Fengle Tower, the palace sightline, the citywide wine network and elite kitchens.← READING ROOM

Architecture is evidence here, not atmosphere. Meng names thresholds, circulation, private rooms, lighting, labor and vertical views, then turns the chapter outward into an urban wine-supply hierarchy.

1. The corridor is a working stage.

PRIMARY TEXT13A / RENDIAN
凡京師酒店，門首皆縛綵樓歡門。唯任店入其門，一直主廊約百餘步，南北天井兩廊皆小閤子。向晚燈燭熒煌，上下相照，濃妝妓女數百，聚於主廊槏面上，以待酒客呼喚，望之宛若神仙。
WORKING TRANSLATION

In the capital, wine houses generally set up decorated cailou and huanmen at their entrances. At Rendian, once inside the gate, a main corridor ran straight for more than a hundred paces; on both the north and south sides, courtyards and flanking corridors contained small private rooms. Toward evening the lamps and candles shone brightly above and below. Meng says that several hundred heavily made-up women gathered along the open, door-and-window frontage of the main corridor, waiting to be summoned by drinkers; from a distance, he says, they looked like immortals.

Text locks: 小閤子 · 槏面Translation: Galok / working translation
TERM / 槏面

槏 is glossed in traditional lexicography as a door or a post beside a window, and the later Mengliang lu repeats the same wine-house phrase. The translation therefore uses “open / door-and-window frontage” while leaving the exact architectural form unresolved.

2. Fengle Tower turns height into spectacle.

PRIMARY TEXT13B / CRAFT STREETS + FENGLE TOWER
北去楊樓，以北穿馬行街，東西兩巷，謂之大小貨行，皆工作伎巧所居。小貨行通雞兒巷妓館，大貨行通牋紙店。白礬樓，後改為豐樂樓。宣和間，更修三層相高，五樓相向，各有飛橋欄檻，明暗相通，珠簾繡額，燈燭晃耀。
WORKING TRANSLATION

North past Yanglou and across Maxing Street were two east-west lanes known as the Large and Small Goods Rows, occupied by skilled craftspeople. The Small Goods Row connected with the houses of sex work in Ji’er Lane, while the Large Goods Row connected with paper shops. White Alum Tower was later renamed Fengle Tower. During the Xuanhe period it was rebuilt with three levels rising above one another and five towers facing each other, linked by flying bridges with railings. Light and shadow passed between them; pearl curtains, embroidered plaques and brilliant lamps filled the complex.

Text lock: 飛橋欄檻Translation: Galok / working translation
PRIMARY TEXT13C / LANTERNS + PALACE SIGHTLINE
初開數日，每先到者賞金旗，過一兩夜則已。元夜則每一瓦隴中皆置蓮燈一盞。內西樓後來禁人登眺，以第一層下視禁中。大抵諸酒肆瓦市，不以風雨寒暑，白晝通夜，駢闐如此。
WORKING TRANSLATION

For the first few days after it opened, those who arrived first were rewarded with golden banners; after a night or two this stopped. On the Lantern Festival, a lotus lantern was placed in every roof ridge. Later, people were forbidden to climb the inner western tower because even its first level looked down into the palace precinct. In general, wine houses and entertainment markets were crowded like this regardless of wind, rain, heat or cold, through the day and into the night.

Siku text / Wikisource ↗Translation: Galok / working translation
GALOK READING

The same verticality that made Fengle Tower spectacular also created a political problem: one tower gave a downward view into the palace precinct. The source records the later restriction on climbing it.

3. The wine house belongs to a distribution system.

PRIMARY TEXT13D / ZHENGDIAN + JIAODIAN
州東宋門外仁和店、姜店，州西宜城樓、藥張四店、班樓，金梁橋下劉樓，曹門蠻王家、乳酪張家，州北八仙樓，戴樓門張八家園宅正店，鄭門河王家、李七家正店，景靈宮東墻長慶樓。在京正店七十二戶，此外不能遍數，其餘皆謂之腳店。
WORKING TRANSLATION

Among the named establishments were Renhe and Jiang shops outside Song Gate east of the prefectural city; Yicheng Tower, Yao Zhang’s Fourth Shop and Banlou to the west; Liulou below Jinliang Bridge; the Man Wang and Rulao Zhang houses at Caomen; Baxianlou to the north; Zhang Ba’s garden-residence zhengdian at Dailou Gate; the He Wang and Li Qi zhengdian at Zheng Gate; and Changqinglou by the east wall of Jingling Palace. Meng reports seventy-two zhengdian households in the capital. Beyond these he could not list them all; the remaining wine shops were called jiaodian.

Institutional note: 正店 / 腳店 is not merely “large / small restaurant”Translation: Galok / working translation
INSTITUTION / 正店—腳店

Research on Northern Song alcohol administration connects the distinction to brewing rights and downstream retail supply. Prestige and scale could differ, but “big restaurant versus small restaurant” is too weak a definition.

4. Elite kitchens and signs fill the street.

PRIMARY TEXT13E / ELITE KITCHENS + STREETSCAPE
賣貴細下酒、迎接中貴飲食，則第一白廚，州西安州巷張秀，以次保康門李慶家、東雞兒巷郭廚、鄭皇后宅後宋廚、曹門塼筒李家、寺東骰子李家、黃胖家。九橋門街市酒店，綵樓相對，繡旆相招，掩翳天日。政和後來，景靈宮東墻下長慶樓尤盛。
WORKING TRANSLATION

For expensive and refined dishes to accompany wine, and for meals serving palace-connected elites, the foremost was Bai Chu. Next came Zhang Xiu in Anzhou Lane west of the prefectural city, the Li Qing household at Baokang Gate, Guo Chu in East Ji’er Lane, Song Chu behind Empress Zheng’s residence, the Li family at Zhuantong near Caomen, the Dice Li family east of the monastery, and the Huang Pang household. Along the streets by the nine bridge gates, wine houses faced one another with decorated towers and embroidered banners so dense they seemed to screen the sky. After the Zhenghe period, Changqinglou beneath the east wall of Jingling Palace was especially prosperous.
