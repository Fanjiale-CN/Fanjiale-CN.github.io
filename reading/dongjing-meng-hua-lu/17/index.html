17 / 大內前州橋東街巷

The guesthouse corridor turns a route into an arrival district, then hands the reader to Xiangguo Temple.← VOLUME III

The most important sentence in Entry 17 identifies both a lodging corridor and its users. That turns a sequence of place-names into evidence about how travellers entered and temporarily inhabited the capital.

1. Xiangguo Temple is already in view.

PRIMARY TEXT17A / APPROACH
大內前州橋之東，臨汴河大街，曰相國寺，有橋平正如州橋，與保康門相對。橋西賈家瓠羹、孫好手饅頭，近南即保康門潘家黃耆圓。延寧宮禁，女道士觀，人罕得入。街西保康門瓦子。
WORKING TRANSLATION

East of Zhouqiao before the palace runs the main street along the Bian River, by Xiangguo Temple. A level bridge there, similar to Zhouqiao, faces Baokang Gate. West of the bridge are Jia Family’s calabash soup and Sun Haoshou’s steamed buns; near Baokang Gate is Pan Family’s Huangqi Pill shop. Yanning Gong is a restricted women’s Daoist establishment that few people may enter. West of the street is the Baokang Gate wazi.

延寧宮: restricted women’s Daoist establishment; do not overdefine “禁”Translation: Galok / working translation

2. The wall becomes a lodging corridor.

PRIMARY TEXT17B / GUESTHOUSES
東去沿城皆客店，南方官員、商賈、兵級，皆於此安泊。近東四聖觀、襪袎巷。
WORKING TRANSLATION

Eastward along the city wall are guesthouses. Officials, merchants and military personnel from the south lodge here. Nearby to the east are Sisheng Guan and Wayao Lane.

安泊 = lodge / stay · 襪袎巷 preferred by Siku and transmitted witnessesTranslation: Galok / working translation

GALOK READING

This is commercial urban lodging, distinct from the official diplomatic hostel in Entry 16. Kaifeng also had government-managed rental property, but Meng does not identify the ownership of these guesthouses. Galok leaves that question open.

3. The route keeps layering memory, education and religion.

PRIMARY TEXT17C / EASTWARD
以東城角定力院，內有朱梁高祖御容。出保康門外，新建三尸廟、德安公廟。南至橫街，西去通御街，曰麥稍巷口。以南太學東門、水櫃街、余家染店。以南街東法雲寺。又西去橫街、張駙馬宅。寺南佑神觀後門。
WORKING TRANSLATION

Farther east at the city corner is Dingli Yuan, which contains a portrait of the founder of Later Liang. Outside Baokang Gate are newly built religious sites. Southward the route reaches a cross street, then the eastern gate of the Imperial Academy, Shuigui Street, Yu Family Dye Shop, Fayun Temple, a princess-consort residence and the rear gate of Youshen Guan.

URBAN MEMORY / the Later Liang portrait predates the Song dynastyTranslation: Galok / working translation
