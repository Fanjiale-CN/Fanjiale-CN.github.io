DONGJING MENG HUA LU / 卷四

軍頭司

The city in uniform.

Meng begins Volume IV with a military catalogue that keeps changing function as it unfolds. Guards train and fight; other units ride, play music, carry imperial objects, build, repair, supply, sweep and serve. The roster is also chronologically layered, so this page treats it as remembered urban capacity rather than a single frozen organization chart.

00 / VOLUME IV OPENS

Uniform does not mean one job.

守 · 閱 · 樂 · 載 · 作 · 供 · 掃

Entry 28 begins with combat inspection and daily drill, then spreads outward into court ceremony, transport, workshops, food supply and maintenance. In Meng's memory, military registration reaches deep into the practical machinery of the capital.

The final phrase, 「記省不盡」, also sets the epistemic tone: this is a recalled list with acknowledged limits.

02 / URBAN STATE

The roster spans more than one city-state.

Entry 28 cannot safely be plotted as a single-date table. Several units were created, reorganized or abolished during Meng's lifetime; at least one name raises a post-Northern-Song problem in the transmitted text.

1104CREATED

廣固 was established under Huizong for capital works.

1115EXPANDED

Guanggu staffing was increased, reinforcing its role in urban construction labor.

1120REORGANIZED

The old 六軍 and selected Six Armies were merged into Guangxiao, so the remembered label carries a date problem after this point.

1125ABOLISHED

清衛 was abolished late in the Northern Song, giving the roster another visible chronological seam.

POST-1127?TEXT PROBLEM

銀槍班 is recorded in the dynastic military history as a Southern Song creation. Its presence here requires collation, not silent harmonization.

A remembered roster of soldiers, performers, carriers, builders and service labor.↗ CAPABILITY MAP↗ TEXT PROBLEMS

1. The chapter opens with inspection and performance of skill.

PRIMARY TEXT28A / INSPECTION
軍頭司每旬休，按閱內等子、相撲手、劍棒手格鬥。
WORKING TRANSLATION

On the ten-day rest cycle, the Military Head Office inspected palace guards, wrestlers and sword-and-staff fighters in combat practice.

GALOK READING

Volume IV therefore begins with bodies under inspection: skill is watched, tested and displayed before the catalogue expands into the city's wider institutional capacity.

PRIMARY TEXT28B / PALACE + FIELD DRILL
諸軍營：殿前指揮使直在禁中，有左右班。內殿直、散員、散都頭、散直、散指揮。御龍左右直（係打御從物）、御龍骨朵子直、弓箭直、弩直。習馭直、騎御馬。鈞容直。招箭班、金槍班、銀槍班、殿侍諸軍東西五班、常入祗候。每日教閱野戰。每遇諸路解到武藝人，對御格鬥。
WORKING TRANSLATION

Meng moves rapidly through palace guards, imperial attendants, archers, crossbowmen, horse units, military musicians and weapon specialists. Daily field drill and martial demonstrations before the emperor keep the opening emphasis on practiced performance.

SOURCE CALIBRATION

The name 銀槍班 creates a chronology problem. The modern annotated edition flags it as likely Southern Song; the site therefore keeps the transmitted reading visible and marks the contradiction.

PRIMARY TEXT28C / SCALE
天武、捧日、龍衛、神衛，各二十指揮，謂之「上四軍」，不出戍。驍騎、雲騎、拱聖、龍猛、龍騎，各十指揮。
WORKING TRANSLATION

The Tianwu, Pengri, Longwei and Shenwei were each remembered as twenty commands and called the Upper Four Armies. Meng then lists further cavalry formations as ten commands each.

NUMBER CAUTION

These numbers belong to Meng's remembered roster. Modern institutional records do not align neatly with every figure, so the page keeps them as textual claims rather than calculating a total garrison population.

PRIMARY TEXT28D / FROM ARMY TO URBAN LABOR
殿前司、步軍司有虎翼，各二十指揮；虎翼水軍、宣武，各十五指揮。神勇、廣勇，各十指揮。飛山、床子弩、雄武、廣固等指揮。諸司則宣效、六軍、武肅、武和、街道司諸司。諸軍指揮，動以百數。
WORKING TRANSLATION

The list continues through infantry, water troops, specialist weapons, Guanggu and units attached to offices including the Street Office. By this point the roster is already describing more than battlefield force.

GALOK READING

Street repair, drainage, cleaning and market management sit beside military labels. Uniform becomes one of the ways the capital organizes labor.

PRIMARY TEXT28E / SERVICE MACHINE
其餘工匠：修內司、八作司、廣固作坊、後苑作坊、書藝局、綾錦院、文繡院、內酒坊、法酒庫、牛羊司、油醋庫、儀鸞司、翰林司、喝探、武嚴、輦官、車子院、皇城司親從官、親事官、上下宮、皇城黃皂院子、滌除，各有指揮，記省不盡。
WORKING TRANSLATION

The catalogue ends in workshops, textile production, wine and food supply, tents and ceremonial setup, warning and escort personnel, sedan-chair bearers, vehicle services, palace attendants and cleaning labor. Each had its own command; Meng says his memory cannot exhaust the list.

MEMORY LIMIT

記省不盡 is the most important methodological phrase on the page. The roster declares its own incompleteness.

03 / COLLATION TARGETS

Keep the seams visible.

銀槍班CHRONOLOGY PROBLEM

Dynastic military history places Silver Spear units in the Southern Song. The transmitted reading remains visible pending witness-level collation.

床子弩 / 林子弩WITNESS VARIANT

Historical editions differ at this unit name. Entry 28 should preserve the variant trail instead of normalizing the phrase into one clean label.

皇城司EDITORIAL CORRECTION

The modern annotated edition reports a base-text error at this point and emends 皇城官 to 皇城司. The correction belongs in the source trail.

04 / 1147 MEMORYThe list ends where memory does.

Meng does not close with a total, a table or an institutional diagram. He stops after one more cascade of names and admits that recollection cannot contain them all.

各有指揮，記省不盡。
