雇覓人力

Hiring the city.

In a few lines Meng sketches an urban hiring network. Occupational intermediaries called 行老 supply workers in several service categories; the text separately routes the search for female servants through a 牙人.

PRIMARY TEXT24A / HIRING NETWORK
凡雇覓人力，幹當人、酒食、作匠之類，各有行老供雇。覓女使即有引至牙人。
WORKING TRANSLATION

When people sought workers for hire — including categories such as gandang personnel, food-and-drink service and artisans — each category had hanglao who supplied workers. When seeking a female servant, the customer was instead directed or introduced to a yaren.

GALOK READING

The economic mechanism is intermediation. Demand does not meet labor only by chance on the street; the text places named intermediary roles in between. The female-servant route deserves separate treatment because Meng gives it separately.

TERM LIMIT / INSTITUTIONSKEEP THE HISTORICAL NAMES.

行老 and 牙人 can be described functionally as intermediaries. Their exact institutional, contractual and gendered meanings require more than a modern job-market analogy.
