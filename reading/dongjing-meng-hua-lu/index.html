CITY MEMORY / NORTHERN SONG

東京夢華錄

A city remembered after it was lost.

Meng Yuanlao reconstructed Northern Song Kaifeng from memory after the dynasty fell and he moved south. Galok reads the book as an urban archive: walls, waterways, state logistics, streets, markets, wine houses, food, festivals and ordinary routines.

START WITH 01VOLUME IIISOURCE ROOM
TERM NOTE / 釋名

“Dongjing” (東京) here means the Northern Song Eastern Capital, Bianjing — present-day Kaifeng. It does not refer to Tokyo, Japan. The book was completed in 1147 after Meng Yuanlao had left the fallen capital and was recalling it from the south.

01 / VOLUME ARCHIVE

Ten volumes. Twenty-five entries live.

Volumes I–II establish the city frame and street economy. Volume III now reaches the operating systems beneath daily life: specialists, institutions, retail, freight, money counting, labor matching and fire response.
