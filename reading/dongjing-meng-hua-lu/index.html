CITY MEMORY / NORTHERN SONG

東京夢華錄

A city remembered after it was lost.

Meng Yuanlao reconstructed Northern Song Kaifeng from memory after the dynasty fell and he moved south. The book is less a linear history than an urban inventory: walls, waterways, streets, shops, food, weddings, festivals, crowds and the routines that made the capital feel alive.

TERM NOTE / 釋名

“Dongjing” (東京) here means the Northern Song Eastern Capital, Bianjing — present-day Kaifeng. It does not refer to Tokyo, Japan. The book was completed in 1147, after Meng Yuanlao had left the fallen Northern Song capital and was recalling the city from the south.

Begin with the city's physical frame.

Volume I moves from the outer wall into the old city, waterways, imperial precinct and government offices. Six entries build the spatial skeleton before the book turns toward streets and daily commerce.
