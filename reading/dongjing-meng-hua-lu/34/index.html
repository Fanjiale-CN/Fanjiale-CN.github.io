DONGJING MENG HUA LU / 卷四

筵會假賃

The host only pays.

A Kaifeng banquet is a contracted product. The 茶酒司 rents the furniture and vessels, the 廚司 supplies food and drink, the 白席人 run the floor — together, the 四司人. Crews keep territories, rate schedules and a rule against overcharging. The host's entire job is money.

A banquet as a contracted product: three named bureaus, one unnamed fourth, five delivery clauses.↗ THE CONTRACT↗ TEXT DETECTIVE

1. The banquet breaks into bureaus.

PRIMARY TEXT34A / THE FOUR BUREAUS
凡民間吉凶筵會，椅桌陳設，器皿合盤，酒檐動使之類，自有茶酒司管賃。吃食下酒，自有廚司。以至托盤、下請書、安排坐次、尊前執事、歌說勸酒，謂之「白席人」。總謂之「四司人」。
WORKING TRANSLATION

For ordinary families' banquets, auspicious or funerary, the chairs and tables, the vessels and trays, the wine-load gear and such movable equipment were rented through the 茶酒司. Food and drink came from the 廚司. And the carrying of trays, the delivery of invitations, the arranging of seats, the attendance at the honored place, the singing and the urging of wine — that was the 白席人. Together they were called the 四司人.

GALOK READING

Meng's scope word is 凡民間 — this system belongs to ordinary households, not the court. The division of labor is by object and by motion: one bureau owns the furniture, one owns the food, one owns the floor. The page keeps the fourth slot empty because the text does.

2. The clauses end where the host begins: paying.

PRIMARY TEXT34B / THE CONTRACT
欲就園館亭榭寺院遊賞命客之類，舉意便辦，亦各有地分，承攬排備，自有則例，亦不敢過越取錢。雖百十分，廳館整肅，主人只出錢而已，不用費力。
WORKING TRANSLATION

For garden parties, pavilions, temples or sightseeing with invited guests — the event was arranged the moment it was wanted. Each crew had its own territory, took on the engagement and prepared it, kept to established rate schedules, and dared not charge beyond them. However many hundreds of items a banquet involved, the hall ended orderly — and the host's only task was to pay, with no effort required.

GALOK READING

The final sentence is the entry's thesis. 雖百十分 concedes scale — hundreds of pieces, hundreds of portions — and the answer stays the same: the system absorbs the complexity, the hall comes out orderly, and the host pays. It is a claim about service capacity, not about prices; the entry never prints a single rate.

03 / CONTRACT COMPLETEThe host only pays.

Entry 34 finishes the rental arc with its most abstract product: not a vehicle, not a funeral, not standing labor — a whole event, delivered to contract. The banquet is produced by specialists and governed by schedules; the household's contribution is money.

主人只出錢而已，不用費力。
