1. The exodus.

PRIMARY TEXT51A / GATES / GARDENS / TEMPLES / BOATS
收燈畢，都人爭先出城探春，州南則玉津園外學方池亭榭、玉仙觀，轉龍灣西去一丈佛園子、王太尉園，奉聖寺前孟景初園，四里橋望牛岡劍客廟。自轉龍彎東去陳州門外，園館尤多。州東宋門外快活林、勃臍陂、獨樂岡、硯台、蜘蛛樓、麥家園，虹橋王家園，曹、宋門之間東御苑，乾明崇夏尼寺。州北李駙馬園。州西新鄭門大路，直過金明池西道者院，院前皆妓館。以西宴賓樓有亭榭，曲折池塘鞦韆畫舫，酒客稅小舟，帳設遊賞。相對祥祺觀，直至板橋，有集賢樓、蓮花樓，乃之官河東、陝西五路之別館，尋常餞送，置酒於此。過板橋，有下松園、王太宰園、杏花岡。金明池角南去水虎翼巷水磨下蔡太師園。南洗馬橋西巷內華嚴尼寺、王小姑酒店北金水河兩浙尼寺巴婁寺、養種園，四時花木，繁盛可觀。南去藥梁園、童太師園。南去鐵佛寺、鴻福寺、東西柏榆樹。州北模天坡、角橋至倉王廟、十八壽聖尼寺，孟四翁酒店。州西北元有庶人園，有創台、流杯亭榭數處，放人春賞。大抵都城左近，皆是園圃，百里之內，並無闃地。
WORKING TRANSLATION

When the lanterns were taken in, the people raced from the city gates to seek spring. To the south: the Yujin Garden, the outer school pond with its pavilions, the Yuxian Temple, then west past Longwan to the Yizhang Buddha Garden, the Wang Grand Preceptor Garden, the Meng Jingchu Garden before Fengsheng Temple, the Sili Bridge, the Wangniu Mound and the Sword-Carrier Temple. East from Longwan Bend outside Chenzhou Gate, gardens and pavilions were especially numerous. Outside Song Gate on the east: the Happy Forest, the Boqi Slope, the Sole Joy Mound, the Inkstone, the Spider Tower, the Mai Family Garden, the Wang Family Garden at Rainbow Bridge, the Eastern Imperial Park between Cao and Song Gates, and the Qianming Chongxia Nunnery. To the north: the Li Prince-Consort Garden. On the west, the great road through Xinzheng Gate ran straight past the Jinming Pool to the Daoist Institute on the west, its front lined with singing houses. Further west the Yanbin Tower had pavilions and winding ponds, swings and painted boats, where wine customers hired small boats for their outings. Across from it the Xiangqi Temple, and on to Banqiao, where the Jixian Tower and the Lianhua Tower served as guest lodges for officials posted to Hedong and the five circuits of Shaanxi — ordinary farewell banquets were held here. Past Banqiao: the Lower Pine Garden, the Wang Grand Preceptor Garden, the Apricot Blossom Mound. South of the Jinming Pool corner, the Tiger-Wing Lane water mill, below it the Cai Grand Preceptor Garden. South of the Horse-Washing Bridge, in the lane to the west, the Huayan Nunnery and the Wang Little Sister Wine Shop; north of the Golden Water River, the Liangzhe Nunnery, the Balou Temple, and the Propagation Garden with its four seasons of flowers and trees, splendid to behold. South to the Yao Liang Garden and the Tong Grand Preceptor Garden. South to the Iron Buddha Temple, the Hongfu Temple, and the east and west cypress and elm trees. North of the prefecture, the Motian Slope, the Corner Bridge to the Cangwang Temple, the Eighteen Shousheng Nunnery, and the Meng Siwu Wine Shop. Northwest of the prefecture there had long been the Commoner’s Garden, with its raised terrace and winding-stream pavilions, opened for spring viewing. In general, all the land around the capital was gardens and groves — within a hundred li, Meng says, scarcely anywhere lay deserted.

GALOK READING

The exit from the city gates is a geography lesson delivered as a directory. Each direction is annotated with garden names, temple names, wine shop names, boat rental references — the metropolitan fringe as a highly heterogeneous space of old imperial gardens, decayed estates, private compounds, temples, singing houses and boat rentals. The page maps them topologically: direction, gate, road, garden. No coordinates, no boundaries — only the relational order the text provides.

2. Spring arrives, and the book hands off.

PRIMARY TEXT51B / SEASON / QINGMING HANDOFF
次第春容滿野，暖律暄晴，萬花爭出，粉牆細柳，斜籠綺陌，香輪暖輾，芳草如茵，駿騎驕嘶，杏花如繡，鶯啼芳樹，燕舞晴空，紅妝按樂於寶榭層樓，白面行歌近畫橋流水，舉目則鞦韆巧笑，觸處則蹴踘鰲狂，尋芳選勝，花絮時墜，金樽折翠簪紅，蜂蝶暗隨歸騎，於是相繼清明節矣。
WORKING TRANSLATION

Then spring’s face filled the countryside; the warming air turned sunny; ten thousand flowers raced out; willows by the whitewashed walls trailed slantwise across the fine paths; fragrant carriage wheels rolled warm over the grass like cushions; spirited horses neighed proudly; apricot blossom was embroidered across the branches; orioles sang in the fragrant trees; swallows danced in the clear sky; robed figures made music in the storied pavilions and tiered towers; pale-faced youths walked singing by the painted bridges and flowing water; everywhere one looked were swinging frames and artful laughter, everywhere football kicked in wild delight; seeking fragrance and choosing the finest views, flower petals sometimes falling, golden goblets, snapped green sprigs, red blossoms tucked in hair, bees and butterflies secretly following the returning riders — and then, one after another, they came to the Qingming Festival.

GALOK READING

The closing paragraph is the book’s most lyrical — and the page lets it be. The season handoff is EDITORIAL / CROSS-ENTRY READING: the entry does not state that Volume VII follows; it simply ends with the word Qingming, and the reader turns the page. 03 / TEXT DETECTIVE

The map is relational. The claim is Meng’s.

闃地 ↔ 闲地COLLATION / SIKU

The Siku witness prints 闃地 (deserted land); later texts print 闲地. The page follows the Siku reading.

庶人園COMPARATIVE / YICHUN TRADITION

The Commoner’s Garden is identified with the old Yichun Garden tradition (葉夢得《石林燕語》); the name carries Qin-prince history and the garden had visibly decayed.

稅小舟HIGH / COMMERCIAL LEISURE

Renting small boats. Commercial leisure rental — not tourism industry, not boat-ticket economy.
