DONGJING MENG HUA LU / 卷四

修整雜貨及齋僧請道

Labor stands at the bridge.

Two needs — a house to repair, a memorial offering to hold — funnel into one morning crowd. At bridge markets and street-lane mouths, wood and bamboo craftsmen, general laborers, Daoist priests and monks stand arrayed, waiting to be hired. Meng names the scene 羅齋.

01 / THE MORNING POOL

Roles stand in rows, waiting to be called.

Every early morning the same cast gathers at the bridge mouths and lane entrances. Meng's own definition is built into the name: 羅立會聚，候人請喚 — standing arrayed and gathered, waiting for a household to call.

1. The bridge mouth is a hiring market without middlemen.

PRIMARY TEXT33A / THE MORNING POOL
倘欲修整屋宇，泥補牆壁，生辰忌日，欲設齋僧尼道士，即早辰橋市街巷口皆有木竹匠人，謂之雜貨工匠，以至雜作人夫，道士僧人，羅立會聚，候人請喚，謂之「羅齋」。竹木作料，亦有鋪席。磚瓦泥匠，隨手即就。
WORKING TRANSLATION

If you wanted a house repaired or walls patched — or, on a birthday or a death-day, a vegetarian offering held for monks and priests — then early in the morning the mouths of the bridge markets, streets and lanes already held wood and bamboo craftsmen, called 雜貨工匠, along with general laborers, Daoist priests and monks, standing in rows and gathered, waiting to be called: this was the 羅齋. Bamboo and timber materials had their own shops. Bricklayers and plasterers were done the moment you reached for them.

GALOK READING

Entry 24 documented hiring through intermediaries. Here the market is direct: workers stand, households call. The entry's time marker does more work than any date — 早辰, early morning, is a daily pulse, assembled before the city is fully awake. And the pool handles both needs in one crowd: repair labor standing beside ritual labor, because households summon both at the same hour.

04 / TEXT DETECTIVE

Title and text use two wordings for one offering.

齋僧請道 ↔ 設齋僧尼道士TITLE / BODY

The title says "feeding monks, inviting priests"; the body says "holding a vegetarian offering for monks, nuns and priests." Both forms are preserved rather than harmonized.

早辰TIME FORM

All witnesses print 早辰, not 早晨. The page keeps the transmitted form.

05 / POOL COMPLETEThe bridge mouth is the city's hiring hall.

Entry 33 catches the rental economy in its rawest state: no shop, no price list, no intermediary — just standing labor and a morning crowd. Repair and ritual pool together because households call for both at the same hour.

羅立會聚，候人請喚。
