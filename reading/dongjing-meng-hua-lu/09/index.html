09 / 朱雀門外

The chapter behaves like a long southward walk. Its strongest evidence is not a single monument but the repeated collision of uses along one ceremonial axis.← READING ROOM

Outside Zhuque Gate, the avenue does not simply become “ordinary city.” It remains ceremonial and controlled while absorbing nightlife, residences, education, medicine, examinations, military institutions, religious compounds and everyday commerce.

1. The gate opens directly into nightlife and residence.

PRIMARY TEXT09A / ZHUQUE GATE
出朱雀門東壁，亦人家。東去大街、麥稭巷、狀元樓，餘皆妓館，至保康門街。其御街東朱雀門外，西通新門瓦子以南殺豬巷，亦妓館。以南東西兩教坊，餘皆居民或茶坊。街心市井，至夜尤盛。
WORKING TRANSLATION

Beyond the eastern side of Zhuque Gate there were also houses. Going east were the main street, Maijie Alley and Zhuangyuan Tower; much of the rest consisted of courtesan establishments stretching toward Baokang Gate Street. Outside Zhuque Gate on the Imperial Avenue, the western side connected toward the Xinmen entertainment quarter and Shazhu Alley, where courtesan houses also stood. Farther south were the eastern and western teaching wards, while the remaining frontage held residents and tea shops. The market activity in the middle of the street was especially lively at night.

Wikisource / Volume II ↗Translation: Galok / working translation
GALOK READING

Meng crosses a major gate and immediately records residents, tea shops, courtesan houses and a night-active street market. The ceremonial axis therefore continues through a district whose social life is neither quiet nor exclusively official.

2. After Longjin Bridge, control returns beside schools and public medicine.

PRIMARY TEXT09B / BRIDGE + SCHOOLS
過龍津橋南去，路心又設朱漆杈子，如內前。東劉廉訪宅，以南太學、國子監。過太學，又有橫街，乃太學南門。街南熟藥惠民南局。以南五里許，皆民居。
WORKING TRANSLATION

South of Longjin Bridge, vermilion barriers were again set in the center of the road, as before the palace. To the east stood the residence of Liu the surveillance official; farther south were the Imperial Academy and the Directorate School. Beyond the academy a cross street reached its south gate. South of that street stood the Southern Bureau for Prepared Medicines and Public Relief. For roughly five li beyond, the district was residential.

Chinese Text Project / Volume II ↗Translation: Galok / working translation
GALOK READING

The crucial detail is the return of the vermilion barriers. Commercial and residential openness outside the palace does not erase controlled movement along the axis. Education and state medicine are embedded in the same corridor.

惠民
STATE MEDICINE / 熟藥惠民南局

A government medicine outlet has a street address.

The institution belongs to the Northern Song system for preparing and selling medicines. On this page it matters spatially: Meng locates a state medical node directly south of the Imperial Academy complex, not in an abstract administrative list.

3. Exams, storage, ritual and royal landscape occupy the same southern corridor.

PRIMARY TEXT09C / INSTITUTIONS + YINGXIANG POOL
又東去橫大街，乃五嶽觀後門。大街約半里許，乃看街亭，尋常車駕行幸，登亭觀馬騎於此。東至貢院、什物庫、禮部、貢院車營務、草場。街南葆真宮，直至蔡河雲騎橋。御街至南薰門裏街西五嶽觀，最為雄壯。自西門東去觀橋、宣泰橋，柳陰牙道，約五里許，內有中太一宮、佑神觀。街南明麗殿、奉靈園。九成宮內安頓九鼎。近東即迎祥池，夾岸垂楊，菰蒲蓮荷，鳧雁遊泳其間，橋亭臺榭，棋布相峙，唯每歲清明日放萬姓燒香遊觀一日。
WORKING TRANSLATION

Another cross street to the east reached the rear gate of the Five Sacred Peaks Abbey. About half a li along the main street stood the Reviewing-Street Pavilion, where the imperial carriage would stop on excursions so the ruler could watch mounted displays. To the east were the examination compound, a miscellaneous-goods storehouse, the Ministry of Rites, the examination transport office and fodder grounds. South of the street stood Baozhen Palace, extending toward Yunqi Bridge over the Cai River. Near Nanshun Gate, the Five Sacred Peaks Abbey on the west side of the Imperial Avenue was especially imposing. Eastward from its west gate ran bridges and a willow-shaded avenue for roughly five li, containing additional religious institutions, palace halls and gardens. The Jiucheng Palace housed the Nine Tripods. Nearby lay Yingxiang Pool, lined with willows, reeds and lotus, with ducks and geese on the water and bridges, pavilions, terraces and halls distributed along its banks. Only on Qingming each year were ordinary people admitted for incense offerings and a day of sightseeing.

Chinese Text Project / Volume II ↗Translation: Galok / working translation
GALOK READING

This section is why the avenue should not be reduced to one label. Examination administration, storage, religious compounds, imperial spectacle and landscaped leisure all attach to the same southward system. Yingxiang Pool adds a controlled form of public access: Meng specifies one annual day when the population could enter for worship and sightseeing.

4. The final approach mixes military institutions, religion, food and summer leisure.

PRIMARY TEXT09D / MILITARY + LEISURE
龍津橋南西壁鄧樞密宅，以南武學巷內曲子張宅、武成王廟。以南張家油餅、明節皇后宅。西去大街，曰大巷口。又西曰清風樓酒店，都人夏月多乘涼於此。以西老鴉巷口軍器所，直接第一座橋。自大巷口南去延真觀，延接四方道民於此。以南西去小巷口三學院，西去直抵宜男橋小巷，南去即南薰門。
WORKING TRANSLATION

On the west side south of Longjin Bridge stood the residence of Privy Councillor Deng. Farther south, inside Military Academy Alley, were the residence of Quzi Zhang and the Temple of King Wucheng. Beyond them were Zhang's fried-cake shop and the residence known as that of Empress Mingjie. A road west led to Daxiangkou, and farther west stood the Qingfeng Tower wine house, where city residents often went to cool themselves in summer. At Laoya Alley stood a weapons office extending toward the first bridge. South from Daxiangkou was Yanzhen Abbey, which received Daoist clergy arriving from different regions. Farther south were the Three Academies and lanes running toward Yinan Bridge; continuing south reached Nanshun Gate.

Chinese Text Project / Volume II ↗Translation: Galok / working translation
GALOK READING

A military academy, weapons office and religious reception point do not displace food and leisure. Zhang's fried cakes and Qingfeng Tower remain part of the same remembered geography. Institutional density and ordinary consumption keep sharing frontage.

TEXT NOTE / 明節皇后宅

One commonly circulated transcription gives 「明節皇太宅」. Other witnesses, including the Chinese Text Project text, give 「明節皇后宅」, matching the posthumous title “Empress Mingjie.” Galok uses 皇后宅 here and keeps the variant visible.

5. At Nanshun Gate, hierarchy becomes a traffic rule.

PRIMARY TEXT09E / NANSHUN GATE
其門尋常士庶殯葬車輿，皆不得經由此門而出，謂正與大內相對，唯民間所宰豬，須從此入京，每日至晚，每群萬數，止十數人驅逐，無有亂行者。
WORKING TRANSLATION

Ordinary funeral carriages and hearses were not permitted to leave the city through this gate, because it directly faced the imperial palace. Pigs slaughtered for the city's population, however, were required to enter through it. Meng says that by evening they arrived in immense groups, driven by only a small number of people, yet without disorder.

Wikisource / Volume II ↗Translation: Galok / working translation
GALOK READING

The final detail is unusually concrete. The gate's symbolic relationship to the palace restricts funeral traffic, while the city's food supply gives slaughtered pigs a compulsory route inward. Ceremonial hierarchy and urban logistics occupy the same gate, but they receive opposite permissions.

METHOD / AXIS, NOT MAP

The central red line encodes the textual direction of travel. It does not claim exact modern coordinates for every institution or alley.

CONTROL DOES NOT DISAPPEAR

The return of vermilion barriers south of Longjin Bridge is retained as physical evidence of regulated movement along the ceremonial avenue.

END OF THE AXIS / 南薰門

Meng ends a long ceremonial corridor with two practical movement rules.

FUNERAL TRAFFIC / OUTBOUND
不得由此門出

Ordinary funeral vehicles were barred from leaving through the gate because it faced the imperial palace.

LIVESTOCK TRAFFIC / INBOUND
豬須從此入京

The city's pigs were required to enter here. The same gate that excluded one kind of traffic concentrated another.
