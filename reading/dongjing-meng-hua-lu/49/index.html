PRIMARY TEXT49A / ONE SENTENCE
十五日詣上清宮，亦有對御，至晚回內。
WORKING TRANSLATION

On the fifteenth day the emperor went to Shangqing Palace; there was also an imperial banquet; by evening he returned inside.

GALOK READING

After the procession’s dense visual inventory, Meng gives the fifteenth a single sentence. The emperor went to a Daoist palace, held the same banquet, and returned. The brevity is the entry’s own statement about where the festival’s center of gravity lay.
