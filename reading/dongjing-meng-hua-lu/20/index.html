20 / 上清宮

Entry 20 is a directory chapter. Its force comes from distribution: named religious institutions are threaded through gates, streets and lanes across the capital.← VOLUME III

Entry 19 moved outward from Xiangguo Temple by street sequence. Entry 20 changes scale again. Meng compresses the city into a list of institutions and location phrases. The result is religious geography without a survey.

1. The primary text reads like an urban directory.

PRIMARY TEXT20A / SHANGQING PALACE DIRECTORY
上清宮，在新宋門裏街北，以西茆山下院。醴泉觀，在東水門裏。觀音院，在舊宋門後太廟南門。景德寺，在上清宮背，寺前有桃花洞，皆妓館。開寶寺，在舊封丘門外斜街子，內有二十四院，惟仁王院最盛。天清寺，在州北清暉橋。興德院，在金水門外。長生宮，在鹿家巷。顯寧寺，在炭場巷。北婆臺寺，在陳州門裏。兜率寺，在紅門道地。踴佛寺，在州西草場巷街。南十方靜因院，在州西油醋巷。浴室院，在第三條甜水巷。福田院，在舊曹門外。報恩寺，在卸鹽巷。太和宮女道士，在州西洪橋子大街。洞元觀女道士，在班樓北。瑤華宮，在金水門外。萬壽觀，在舊酸棗門外十王宮前。
WORKING TRANSLATION

Shangqing Palace was north of the street inside New Song Gate; to its west was the lower court of Maoshan. Liquan Guan was inside East Water Gate. Guanyin Yuan was behind Old Song Gate, by the south gate of the imperial ancestral temple. Jingde Temple stood behind Shangqing Palace; in front of it was Peach-Blossom Cave, occupied by courtesan houses. Kaibao Temple stood on the diagonal street outside Old Fengqiu Gate and contained twenty-four courts, of which Renwang Yuan was the most prominent. The list continues across Qinghui Bridge, Jinshui Gate, Lujia Lane, Tanchang Lane, Chenzhou Gate, western lanes, Sweetwater Lane, Old Caomen and other anchors, ending with Taihe Palace and Dongyuan Guan associated with women Daoists, Yaohua Palace, and Wanshou Guan outside Old Suanzao Gate in front of Shiwang Palace.

Working punctuation follows the common transcription, with the Dongyuan Guan phrase adjusted from the Siku comparison and flagged below.Translation: Galok / working translation
GALOK READING

The chapter's value lies in plurality and distribution. Religious institutions are remembered through ordinary urban anchors — gates, bridges, lanes and commercial streets — rather than isolated in a single sacred precinct.

2. Distribution is evidence; screen position is editorial.

MAP LIMIT / HARD RULE NO INVENTED COORDINATES.

Meng supplies relative location language, but this passage does not provide a measured citywide plan. The constellation above groups names to expose repetition and spread. It should never be read as a georeferenced reconstruction.
