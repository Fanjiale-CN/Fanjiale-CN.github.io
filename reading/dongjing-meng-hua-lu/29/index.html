DONGJING MENG HUA LU / 卷四

皇太子納妃

One wedding, precisely remembered.

Meng gives this ritual only thirty-seven characters. Modern annotation identifies the most plausible event as Crown Prince Zhao Huan's marriage to Lady Zhu in 1116. The page keeps that identification marked as a historical inference while letting the surviving ritual details remain spare.

ENTER 1116 ↓
EVENT IDENTIFICATION1116政和六年

A rare ritual with one strong candidate.

Meng lived in the capital from 1103 until the Jingkang crisis. Yang Chunqiao's annotated edition notes that a crown prince marrying while formally holding that title appears only once in this span: Zhao Huan, made crown prince in 1115, married Lady Zhu in 1116.

The event identification comes from modern historical reconstruction. Meng's thirty-seven-character entry itself supplies ritual form, vehicle and procession detail; it does not name the couple or print the year.

1. The surviving entry is almost pure procession.

PRIMARY TEXT29A / RITUAL + VEHICLE
皇太子納妃，鹵部儀仗，宴樂、儀衛。妃乘厭翟車，車上設紫色團蓋，四柱維幕，四重大帶，四馬駕之。
WORKING TRANSLATION

For the crown prince's marriage, there were the procession register and guards, with banquet music and ceremonial escort. The consort rode in an 厭翟車, covered in purple, with curtains tied to four posts, great belts hanging from the structure, and four horses drawing the carriage.

GALOK READING

The record is dominated by visible form. It reads like a roadside memory of what passed through the city: procession, music, carriage, fabric, structure and horses.

02 / RITUAL OBJECT

厭翟車

The annotated edition connects Meng's vehicle to the ritual tradition preserved in the 《政和五禮新儀》, where the crown-prince marriage rite likewise calls for the consort to ride an 厭翟車.
