DONGJING MENG HUA LU / 卷四

公主出降

Clear the way.

Meng remembers an imperial daughter's wedding through what the street saw: a road watered ahead of the procession, hundreds of dowry platforms, palace women in paired formation, canopies and fans, then an ornate sedan carried by twelve men. The page follows that visible order.

FOLLOW THE PROCESSION ↓
00 / TERM STATE

The title carries its own chronology.

BEFORE 1113公主

The established Northern Song title for an emperor's daughter.

1113–1127帝姬

Huizong changed the official title in 1113; weddings seen in the later capital years could formally be described as 帝姬降嫁.

1147 TEXT公主出降

Meng writes the restored/common title in the finished book. The reason for that wording remains open.

01 / WATERED ROAD

水路

Here 水路 describes street preparation. Several dozen Street Office soldiers move ahead carrying cleaning tools and gilded silver water buckets, sprinkling the road to suppress dust and clear the procession route.

30 / 公主出降

An imperial wedding procession remembered through street work, loads, people, fabric and vehicle construction.↗ PROCESSION↗ TEXT DETECTIVE

1. Street preparation is part of ceremonial display.

PRIMARY TEXT30A / WATERED ROAD
公主出降，亦設儀仗、行幕、步障、水路（凡親王、公主出，則有之，皆係街道司兵級數十人，各執掃具、鍍金銀水桶，前導灑之，名曰「水路」）。
WORKING TRANSLATION

When an imperial daughter married, the procession included ceremonial guards, traveling screens and the so-called 水路. For a prince or princess traveling out, several dozen Street Office soldiers went ahead with cleaning tools and gilded silver water buckets, sprinkling the route.

GALOK READING

The procession begins with municipal labor. Ceremony modifies the street before the central figures arrive.

2. Dowry and attendants create mass before the principal vehicle appears.

PRIMARY TEXT30B / DOWRY + ATTENDANTS
用檐床數百，鋪設房臥，並紫衫卷腳幞頭天武官抬舁。又有宮嬪數十，皆真珠釵插、吊朵、玲瓏簇羅頭面，紅羅銷金袍帔，乘馬雙控雙搭。青蓋前導，謂之「短鐙」。前後用紅羅銷金掌扇遮簇。
WORKING TRANSLATION

Hundreds of carrying platforms bore dowry furnishings and dress, carried by Tianwu soldiers in purple garments and wrapped-foot headwear. Dozens of palace women followed in elaborate jewelry and red-gold robes, riding in pairs, framed by a blue canopy and ceremonial fans.

GALOK READING

Meng remembers density through repeated quantity words: hundreds of loads, dozens of attendants, paired riders, repeated screens and fans.

3. The sedan becomes an object inventory.

PRIMARY TEXT30C / SEDAN
乘金銅檐子，覆以剪棕。朱紅梁脊，上列渗金銅鑄雲鳳花朵。檐子約高五尺許，深八尺，闊四尺許，內容六人。四維垂繡額珠簾，白藤間花。匡箱之外，兩壁出欄檻，皆縷金花，裝雕木人物、神仙。出隊兩竿十二人，竿前後皆設綠絲絛，金魚勾子勾定。
WORKING TRANSLATION

The princess rode in a gold-and-copper sedan covered with trimmed palm fiber. Meng lists vermilion structural members, gilded copper cloud-and-phoenix ornaments, dimensions, bead curtains, gold-thread decoration, carved figures and carrying poles with green cords fixed by gold fish-shaped hooks.

GALOK READING

The detail shifts from public route to crafted surface. The street-scale procession ends in close observation of one vehicle.

04 / TEXT DETECTIVE

Two words change how the procession is imagined.

公主 ↔ 帝姬TERM STATE / 1113

The book's title uses 公主; Huizong's court formally used 帝姬 after 1113. The page preserves both chronological states.

檐床 ↔ 擔床COLLATION PROPOSAL

Deng Zhicheng proposed 擔床. Yang's annotation supports that reading with comparable carrying platforms used for gifts and ceremonial loads.

水路TERM WARNING

The text itself explains this as road sprinkling by Street Office personnel. The page therefore treats it as a prepared street, with no river-route interpretation.

05 / PROCESSION COMPLETEThe road is watered first. The ornament comes last.

Entry 30 turns a royal marriage into an urban sequence: municipal preparation, logistics, personnel and crafted display occupy the same street.
