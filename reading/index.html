05 / HOW WE READ

Five checks before analogy.

  1. TEXT
    What does the source actually say?

    Lock the passage, edition and textual witness before interpretation.

  2. CONTEXT
    What problem existed then?

    Reconstruct the institution, dispute and material conditions around the passage.

  3. MECHANISM
    What relationship can be tested?

    Extract one mechanism rather than turning an old sentence into a universal proverb.

  4. PRESENT
    What survives in modern evidence?

    Build a separate contemporary source trail instead of forcing the past onto the present.

  5. LIMIT
    Where does the analogy break?

    State the institutional differences that make the comparison stop working.
