框 Frame

Concepts that make scattered facts readable.

Frame is for essays that turn messy evidence into a usable structure: an audit, a map, a loop, a spectrum or a question that keeps working after the article ends. The frame should make the reader better at seeing the next case.

Reading lens

A good concept behaves like a tool.

Frame pieces are where Galok builds the portable parts of an argument. They are not meant to make life sound more complicated. They are meant to give ordinary evidence a clearer shape.
