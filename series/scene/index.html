察 Observe

Small scenes that carry larger evidence.

Observe is for close readings of streets, stores, apps, menus, queues, screenshots and habits. The scene matters because abstract change only becomes real when it shows up in a place, a route, a price tag or a repeated gesture.

Reading lens

A small scene can carry the whole system.

Observe pieces begin with something local and visible, then ask what it is evidence of. A cheap coffee order, a ferry view, a crowded tourist street or a quiet receipt can all reveal how value is being negotiated.

Field Streets, storefronts, apps, photos, queues and ordinary routines. Question What does this scene make newly normal or newly doubtful? Result Evidence that keeps analysis from floating away.
