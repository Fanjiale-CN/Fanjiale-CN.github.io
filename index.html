The first issue centers on consumption audit: the moment when price, function, emotion, identity, alternatives, risk and time all appear on the same mental receipt.

Seven lines on the receipt.

A purchase now has to survive several checks before it feels worth it. This is not anti-consumerism. It is desire under verification.

1Price auditCould a credible alternative do this for less?
2Function auditWhat job does it actually perform?
3Emotion auditIs the feeling durable, or just packaging?
4Identity auditWho still reads the signal?
5Alternative auditWhat did apps and peers reveal?
6Risk auditWill this age badly?
7Time auditHow long does the value last?
