What Galok is

A field notebook for visible economics.

Galok is not a news site and not a portfolio. It is a working archive of essays and visual notes about how large forces become small decisions: what people buy, where they queue, which prices feel normal, and which stories stop working.

Essays Long-form arguments grounded in daily evidence. Frames Charts, loops and models that make behavior legible. Scenes Photos and observations from cities, platforms and routines.

Three ways to read ordinary change.

Galok is organized around three editorial lenses. The Chinese glyphs are visual markers; the writing itself stays in English.

视

View

Macro pressure as it becomes visible in daily life.

Policy, balance sheets, prices and confidence, followed back into routine decisions.
框

Frame

Concepts, data and mental models for reading change.

Maps, loops and receipts that turn scattered facts into usable structure.
察

Observe

Street-level evidence from platforms, cities and consumption.

Small scenes that reveal how value, taste and behavior are being renegotiated.

Seven lines on the receipt.

A purchase now has to survive several checks before it feels worth it. This is not anti-consumerism. It is desire under verification.

6Risk auditWill this age badly?
7Time auditHow long does the value last?
