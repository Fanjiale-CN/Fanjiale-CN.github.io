02 THE SYSTEM

The image is not a rectangle.
It is a set of roles.

MixMatter does not decorate a finished photograph. It decides what deserves to stay photographic, what can become field or rhythm, and where the frame needs silence.
01 / IDENTITY

Protect what makes the source itself.

Subject, geometry, decisive relation, gesture, sign, color anchor. The specific evidence survives before anything gets stylized.

02 / HIERARCHY

Give every region a job.

Dominant, supporting, rhythm, field, pause. Repetition stops competing with itself. Empty space becomes active structure.

03 / MATERIAL

Use texture only when structure asks for it.

Halftone, paper, duotone and rupture are consequences, not stickers. Materiality appears where it clarifies separation, compression or emphasis.

05 METHOD

Judgment first.
Surface second.

The interface stays simple. Send an image and say what you want. The system carries the complexity behind that sentence.
  1. 01Read the source

    Separate semantic importance from whatever happens to be visually loud.

    READ
  2. 02Protect identity

    Lock the relationships and details that make this source unmistakably itself.

    PROTECT
  3. 03Rebuild hierarchy

    Assign dominant, supporting, field and quiet roles before styling the surface.

    DIRECT
  4. 04Resolve material

    Move regions between photographic, graphic, printed and collaged states only when useful.

    MATERIALIZE
  5. 05Critique and revise

    Fix the structural cause instead of hiding a weak composition under more texture.

    REVISE
