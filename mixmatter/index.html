02 THE SYSTEM

Protect the source.
Then direct the surface.

MixMatter is not a filter stack. It decides what remains photographic, what becomes graphic, and where the page needs silence.
01 / IDENTITY

Keep the evidence that makes the image itself.

Subject, geometry, decisive relation, color anchor and recognisable detail survive before stylization starts.

02 / HIERARCHY

Assign roles before adding atmosphere.

Dominant, supporting, field and pause. Once the hierarchy is clear, the result feels designed instead of over-processed.

03 / MATERIAL

Texture is a tool, not a sticker pack.

Halftone, paper edges, flat fields and collage seams appear only when they improve separation, compression or emphasis.

05 METHOD

Judgment first.
Surface second.

The interface should stay simple. Send an image, describe the outcome, and let the system carry the complexity behind that sentence.
