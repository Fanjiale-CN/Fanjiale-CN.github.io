THE FIELD / DESIGN LANGUAGE

A quiet field that grows itself.

Galok is a personal magazine built like a field. Its design rules are not decoration: they are the way one person learns to see — and the way that seeing is shared with strangers on the other side of the internet.

01 / PHILOSOPHY

The field grows first. Design serves the growth.

Three laws, one inheritance. Every page, typeface and motion rule below descends from them.

Growth first.

The design should feel like something a plant would choose: unhurried, layered, alive at the edges. Restraint is not emptiness — it is room left for growth.

Design serves information.

Color, type and motion exist to carry an argument, a city or a memory. If a visual cannot be read, it is decoration and decoration is removed.

Restraint by principle.

The palette stays within three colors. Motion stays within one breath. Angles stay right. Discipline is what makes the field legible across years.

02 / PALETTE

Three colors and their weather.

Near-black ink, warm paper and one signal red hold the whole field. Deep blue, gold and green appear only inside their own territories.

ATHE SHARED THREE / 三色共享 — every page carries these three, no exceptions.

BTHE RESERVED THREE / 领域保留 — appear only inside their own territory.

CTHE INK GRAY SCALE / 墨阶辅助 — tints of ink, never a fifth accent.

Tap any swatch to copy its hex. The rule: three colors on every page, never more — the signal red appears once per screen at most.

04 / GRID & RULES

White space is the first material.

Twelve columns hold the desktop; the margins are wide enough to walk in. Hairlines divide without shouting. Every angle is a right angle.

12 / columns

The desktop grid. Cards span six; case studies break the rhythm deliberately, once per page.

48 / 32 px

Content breathing room: 48px gutters at full width, 32px when the view narrows below 640px.

1px / hairline

All dividers are one pixel, in #d8d2c8 or ink at 12%. Never a shadow, never a fill.

NN / naming

Everything is numbered and named: ISSUE 02, CITY 05, PLATE 04. Numbers make the archive searchable by memory.

90° / angles

Corners are right or not at all. The field has no rounded edges — paper is folded, not bent.

05 / MOTION

Less than a breath.

Motion exists only as opacity and transform, never above a quarter of a second, and it keeps silent for anyone who asks for stillness.

Only two properties

opacity + transform. No width, height, top, left — nothing that forces a layout.

≤ 260 ms

A quarter of a second is the ceiling. Reveals settle at 220 ms; presses at 140 ms.

ease-out, gentle

cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1). Things arrive and stop; they never bounce.

Reduced motion

prefers-reduced-motion turns every transition to instant opacity — respect is a design rule.
