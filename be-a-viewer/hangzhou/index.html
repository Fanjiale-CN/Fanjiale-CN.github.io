01 / MEMORY

白居易《忆江南·江南忆》
唐

江南忆，最忆是杭州。
山寺月中寻桂子，
郡亭枕上看潮头。

Of all Jiangnan, Hangzhou returns most clearly: cassia sought by moonlight at the mountain temple; the tide heard from a pillow in the commandery pavilion.

02 / WEATHER

苏轼《饮湖上初晴后雨·其二》
北宋

水光潋滟晴方好，
山色空濛雨亦奇。
欲把西湖比西子，
淡妆浓抹总相宜。

In sun, the water glitters at its best; in rain, the mountains fade into wonder. If West Lake is likened to Xizi, light or lavish dress becomes her equally well.

03 / ABUNDANCE

柳永《望海潮·东南形胜》
北宋

有三秋桂子，十里荷花。
羌管弄晴，菱歌泛夜，
嬉嬉钓叟莲娃。

Cassia through three autumn months; lotus for ten full li. Flutes play into clear weather, water-chestnut songs drift through night, while fishermen and lotus-gatherers take their ease.

Galok translation / 2026中文原文 / Wikisource ↗
04 / WHITE

张岱《湖心亭看雪》
明末清初

雾凇沆砀，
天与云与山与水，
上下一白。

Rime and mist filled the air. Sky, cloud, mountain and water—above and below, everything was white.
