# Hangzhou Field Chapter — Design Record

## Direction

The page moves from **water to city** through five visual distances: Water, Rain, Old City, New City and Night. It treats Hangzhou as a cultural landscape rather than a tourism checklist. West Lake establishes pace and measure; the rest of the city is read outward from it.

## Visual system

- **Mood:** quiet field archive, cool water, measured editorial tension.
- **Palette:** deep water `#0c1719`, paper `#ecece6`, mist `#b8c8c7`, ink `#0b1415`, field red `#e0524d`.
- **Typography:** Galok's existing sans-serif display system; oversized, low-leading chapter headings; compact uppercase metadata.
- **Image language:** all 20 supplied photographs remain visible as photographs. Two source images also receive clearly labelled abstract-editorial derivatives. One new paper poster condenses the chapter into a red causeway, a small lake fragment and generous negative space.
- **Motion:** calm reveal from 24px, one sticky chapter progress line, scroll-snap archive rail, silent field reel with restrained crossfades. All motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Field reel

- Web-native HyperFrames composition, 16:9, 15-second silent loop.
- Five chapters, three seconds each.
- Four photographs per chapter; all 20 supplied photographs appear once.
- Motion is limited to slow scale/position drift and 0.45 second crossfades.
- Typography remains inside a generous editorial safe area.
- No audio, aggressive zoom, wipes, gradients, fake film grain or decorative motion without information value.

## What this design avoids

- No template-like equal card grid in the narrative chapters.
- No invented cultural symbols or pseudo-traditional decoration.
- No hidden source images: the original photographs remain directly accessible in the contact sheet.
- No autoplay sound.
