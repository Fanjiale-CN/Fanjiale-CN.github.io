# 作家笔下的城市 / Cities in Words

Five city pages share one editorial reading system: Chinese source text on the left, a Galok English translation on the right, a running folio, source links, and one city-specific Minimal Zine plate. The composition takes its reading hierarchy from literary journals rather than card grids: issue-like running heads, generous margins, asymmetric folios and long horizontal rules.

## Editorial references

- [The Paris Review](https://www.theparisreview.org/) — long-form literary hierarchy, issue logic and generous whitespace.
- [Granta](https://granta.com/) — writing-first navigation and the proximity of text and art.
- [The Public Domain Review](https://publicdomainreview.org/) — archival source notes and historical works placed in contemporary reading contexts.

## Translation and source policy

Chinese text is checked against the linked work or an institutional reproduction. Classical and public-domain texts can appear at stanza length. Copyrighted modern writing is restricted to brief excerpts. English versions are new Galok translations for this series; they are not copied from published translations.
