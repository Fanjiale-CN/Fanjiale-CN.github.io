# Beijing Visual System

## Style Prompt

An editorial film about Beijing built around the idea of the central axis. Monumental public space, measured palace geometry and intimate side-street life share one visual rhythm. The page moves from ceremony to ordinary motion and then to contemporary form without turning the city into a postcard.

## Design Mode

- Extension of the existing GALOK `BE A VIEWER` system.
- Brand fidelity: high.
- Visual variance: 7 / 10.
- Motion intensity: 6 / 10.
- Content density: 5 / 10.

## Colors

- `#111413` — ink
- `#e8e3d8` — warm stone
- `#f4f1e9` — paper
- `#982f28` — palace vermilion
- `#71817f` — winter jade
- `#aeb7b2` — mist grey

## Typography

- Display and body: the existing Helvetica/Arial-derived GALOK grotesque stack.
- Metadata: uppercase, wide tracking and tabular numerals.
- Hero copy is deliberately brief. Sequence supplies hierarchy before size does.

## Signature Interaction

The `CITY AXIS` is a directly manipulated three-stop rail. Dragging moves the marker one-to-one; releasing snaps to Imperial, Civic or Contemporary Beijing. Buttons and arrow keys provide the same states without drag.

`FRAME THE CITY / 城市取景器` turns observation into authorship. The user opens a virtual camera, moves the viewfinder one-to-one, chooses the postcard ratio and metadata position, then renders the crop locally as a downloadable PNG.

## Motion

- Hero copy follows three video beats: focus pull, editorial push, then stillness.
- The hero carries no decorative crosshair or axis marker; video, copy and playback progress are the only moving layers.
- Section entrances use opacity, transforms and clip-path only.
- The city-axis marker uses direct tracking while held and a short spring-like snap on release.
- The postcard viewfinder tracks the pointer without transition while held. Format and scene changes use short, interruptible state transitions and a single shutter flash.
- Typewriter text runs once when its section enters the viewport.
- Page scroll remains native and never controls media playback position.
- Reduced-motion mode removes transforms, timed copy changes and animated typing.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not pin long sections or create artificial scroll distance.
- Do not continuously scrub video, type or image position from document scroll.
- Do not add generic rounded-card grids, floating blobs or decorative gradients.
- Do not return the hero crosshair or any red marker that competes with the architecture.
- Do not cover important faces or architectural subjects with dense copy.
- Do not autoplay audible media.
