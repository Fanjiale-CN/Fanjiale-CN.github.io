# Shanghai Visual System

## Style Prompt

An architectural field journal about Shanghai: precise, cinematic and editorial. Monumental type is anchored to edges; photographs carry the emotional weight; fine rules, coordinates and chapter numbers supply archival structure. The visual tension is between river haze and electric streets, old stone and new glass, stillness and speed.

## Colors

- `#090b0c` — cinematic black
- `#f2f1ec` — warm paper
- `#38555b` — clouded blue-green
- `#d95b55` — field-note red
- `#c9a15b` — Jing’an gold

## Typography

- Display and body: the site’s existing Helvetica/Arial-derived grotesque stack.
- Metadata: the same family in uppercase, heavy weight, wide tracking and tabular numbers where needed.
- Display tracking stays tight; body copy remains open and readable.

## Motion

- Build final layouts first; animate only opacity, transforms and localized filters.
- Entrances overlap in short editorial sequences, followed by stillness.
- Video text follows three narrative beats and remains independent of page scroll.
- Every animated section has a reduced-motion fallback.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not add scroll locking, scroll-jacking or continuously scrubbed parallax.
- Do not animate layout dimensions or use high-frequency pointer tracking.
- Do not introduce generic rounded cards, neon gradients or floating decorative blobs.
- Do not obscure the primary subject of a photograph with dense copy.
- Do not autoplay audible media.
