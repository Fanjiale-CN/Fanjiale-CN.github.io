01 / INTRODUCTION

Introduction

Xi’an is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in China. Known in earlier periods as Chang’an, it served as the capital of several Chinese dynasties and became the eastern terminus of the ancient Silk Road.

02 / IMPERIAL ORIGIN

The First Capital

Near modern Xi’an, Qin Shi Huang established the first unified imperial state in Chinese history in 221 BCE. The political institutions created during the Qin period shaped the structure of Chinese government for centuries.

03 / ARCHAEOLOGY

Terracotta Army

1974

Discovered in 1974 near the mausoleum of Qin Shi Huang, the Terracotta Army consists of thousands of life-sized soldiers, horses and chariots. Their faces, hairstyles, armour and postures vary, giving the army an extraordinary sense of individuality.

04 / URBAN FORM

City Wall

Xi’an’s surviving city wall was constructed during the Ming dynasty. It forms a complete rectangular circuit around the historic centre and remains one of the best-preserved major city walls in China.

05 / THE CITY NOW

Xi’an Today

Modern Xi’an is a major university, research, aerospace and technology centre. High-speed rail and an expanding metro system connect the historic capital with the rest of China, while its neighbourhoods continue to preserve strong local food and street-life traditions.
