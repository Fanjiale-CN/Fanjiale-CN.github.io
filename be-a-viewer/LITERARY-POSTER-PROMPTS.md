# Minimal Zine poster prompts / Cities in Words

All five images were generated as quiet editorial plates with no large readable typography. The ratio constraint overrides the source skill’s default portrait format: only 1:1 square and 3:2 horizontal are used.

## Hangzhou / 3:2

Minimal Zine Poster v0.1, horizontal literary-art plate on pale celadon fibrous paper: an airy West Lake causeway, one tiny pavilion, faint ripples and misty hills rendered as imperfect black photocopy and pale grey-green screenprint; one thin vermilion route and a small cinnabar seal; archival, understated, more blank space than imagery, no gradients, no polished vector finish.

## Beijing / 1:1

Minimal Zine Poster v0.1, square literary plate on warm wheat paper: fragments of an old Beijing gate, courtyard tree, roofline and a small kite as black rubbing and imperfect photocopy; one palace-vermilion block and seal; dry air, large breathing room, restrained museum-print character, no gradients, no glossy finish.

## Shanghai / 3:2

Minimal Zine Poster v0.1, horizontal modernist literary plate on pearl-grey newsprint: clipped Bund cornice, Art Deco window grid, curved railway line and a distant river horizon in black halftone; one oxidised jade-green block, red registration marks and a small seal; lively but sparse, 1930s print energy without pastiche.

## Xi’an / 1:1

Minimal Zine Poster v0.1, square plate on ochre stele-rubbing paper: layered city-wall merlons, stone inscription textures, one horse-hoof trace and two dry autumn leaves in black charcoal rubbing; one cinnabar line and seal; monumental, time-worn and minimal.

## Xiamen / 3:2

Minimal Zine Poster v0.1, horizontal coastal literary plate on pale sea-glass paper: Gulangyu shoreline, low hill, one small sailboat, shell fragment and weathered veranda in black photocopy and cyan-grey screenprint; a single cobalt tide line and tiny orange-red seal; salt-air correspondence, generous blank space, quiet archival feel.
