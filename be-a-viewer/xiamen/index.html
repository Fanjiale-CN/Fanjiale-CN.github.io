01 / SEA LETTER

鲁迅《厦门通信》
1926

这楼就在海边，
日夜被海风呼呼地吹着。

The building stands right beside the sea, buffeted day and night by the rush of the sea wind.

02 / CHANGING LIGHT

鲁迅《厦门通信》
1926

山光海气，
是春秋早暮都不同。

Mountain light and sea air are never the same from spring to autumn, from morning to evening.

03 / SAILS

鲁迅《厦门通信》
1926

许多小船，吃水很重，
都张着帆驶向鼓浪屿去。

Many small boats, riding deep in the water, spread their sails and made for Gulangyu.

Galok translation / public domain中文原文 / Wikisource ↗
04 / ISLAND RECORD

周凯 纂辑《厦门志》
清

鼓浪屿在海中，
上有小山、民居、田园、村舍；
负山临海，舟可直抵其下。

Gulangyu lies in the sea, with low hills, homes, fields and village houses; it rests against the hills and faces the water, where boats may come directly ashore.

Galok translation / historical gazetteer中文原文 / Wikisource ↗
