01 / TIDE TABLE

MOVE THE
COASTLINE.

Xiamen is read between land and water.

The city rarely settles into one identity. A ferry wake becomes a street rhythm; a red roof becomes a horizon marker; temple colour returns in a shopfront.

03 / GULANGYU

AN ISLAND
AT WALKING SPEED.

No cars. Every turn is measured by shade, steps and sea air.

Gulangyu compresses distance. Villas, gardens and harbour views arrive within a few minutes of one another, yet the island rewards anyone willing to slow down.
