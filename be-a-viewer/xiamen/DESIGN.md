# Xiamen — Tide Viewfinder

## Style prompt

An editorial coastal field note shaped by Xiamen's sea light and Minnan roof culture. The page should feel like a tide moving through a contact sheet: cinematic at entry, tactile in the middle, quiet at departure.

## Visual identity

- Deep sea ink `#092b32` anchors video and immersive sections.
- Minnan brick red `#a83f2f` carries chapter markers and the coastal table.
- Shell paper `#f6f3ea` gives long reading sections a warm, non-clinical ground.
- Sea glass `#78aaa7` and jade `#2e6f64` appear as secondary information colours.
- Display type stays within GALOK's neo-grotesque system; utility labels use tight uppercase tracking.

## Motion

- Hero footage crossfades through three fixed-size scenes: water, stone and city.
- Copy enters after the image with a short vertical reveal.
- The tide table, roof notes and street seam respond directly to user input.
- Mobile scroll never drives image transforms, pinning or frame-by-frame video.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; videos pause and reveals become immediate.

## Interaction rules

- All controls have a minimum 44px target.
- The island gallery supports native touch scroll and mouse drag.
- The tide and street range controls remain keyboard operable.
- Video autoplay failure leaves a usable photographic poster and play control.

## Avoid

- Generic cyan gradients.
- Decorative motion without an editorial purpose.
- Long pinned sections or scroll-jacked timelines.
- Floating text that ignores the photographed architecture.
