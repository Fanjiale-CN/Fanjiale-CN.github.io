Press-Print / Legal

Privacy Policy

Effective 9 September 2026 · Published by Fan Jiale / Galok

1. Scope

Press-Print is an image-focused Agent Skill and Skills-only Plugin. This policy describes the data handling performed by Press-Print itself.

2. No independent backend

Press-Print does not operate its own user account system, authentication service, MCP server, database, advertising system, or external image-processing backend. The Skill consists of instructions and supporting files used inside a compatible host environment such as ChatGPT or Codex.

3. Images, prompts, and outputs

Press-Print itself does not independently transmit user-supplied images, prompts, or generated outputs to Fan Jiale / Galok. When Press-Print is invoked in a host product, that product processes the conversation, uploaded files, model requests, and outputs according to its own terms, privacy policy, account settings, and data controls.

Installing or invoking the Skills-only package does not create a separate Press-Print channel that sends conversation or image contents to the publisher.

4. Data collected by Press-Print

Press-Print does not intentionally maintain a separate database of names, contact information, uploaded photographs, prompts, conversation transcripts, generated images, payment information, precise location, authentication credentials, or sensitive personal data.

5. Website and repository

Documentation and policy pages may be hosted on galok.me and GitHub. Visiting those services may cause standard web request metadata to be processed by their hosting or infrastructure providers. That website traffic is separate from execution of the Press-Print Skill inside ChatGPT or Codex.

GitHub Issues are public by default. Do not post private images, credentials, or sensitive personal information in a public issue.

6. Third-party host platforms

Press-Print may be distributed through third-party platforms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Press-Print does not control how a host platform stores, retains, trains on, or otherwise processes content submitted directly to that platform.

7. Children

Press-Print does not knowingly operate a separate service directed at children or independently collect children's personal information. Eligibility to use a host platform is governed by that platform's terms.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated if Press-Print's architecture or distribution model changes. A material change, such as adding an external server or independent data collection, should be disclosed before or when that functionality is introduced.

9. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to galokview@outlook.com. The public source repository is Fanjiale-CN/press-print.
