01Editorial system / 2026

GALOK FIELD NOTES

One magazine, several ways of seeing.

The site had to hold essays, city records, moving image and printed editions without forcing every format into the same page. A shared editorial spine now carries navigation, type, captions and archive relationships while each subject keeps its own visual temperature.

Intent
Make a mixed personal practice legible as one publication.
Role
Research, writing, art direction, interface design and implementation.
Result
A public archive with stable routes, five open city records and twelve essays.

02City archive / 2026

CITIES / BE A VIEWER

Let each city keep its weather.

Beijing, Shanghai, Xi’an, Xiamen and Hangzhou use different colour, pacing and image logic. Their shared chapter structure, literary spreads and return paths make the series readable as one body of work.

Intent
Record cities without flattening them into a tourism template.
Material
Photography, film, literature, field captions and editorial editions.
Decision
Consistency lives in structure; atmosphere remains city-specific.

03Data reading / 2026

DATA / CHINA

Read the gap between headline and household.

The data desk places growth beside household pressure, employment, property, local finance and outside institutional readings. Interactive charts appear where comparison helps; source notes remain attached to every claim.

Intent
Show what one national number cannot explain on its own.
Method
Source comparison, editorial synthesis, chart design and responsive longform.
Rule
Every visual must advance the reading or leave the page.
