# Galok About — Visual specification

## Mode

Redesign · Overhaul. Preserve `/about/`, the global navigation, the Galok symbol and lockup, the existing city and film assets, `#about-main`, `#field-film`, analytics, contact details, and reduced-motion support.

## Design read

- Artifact: creator / visual archive About page
- Audience: visitors entering Galok through city work, essays, design work, or data
- Visual language: quiet luxury furniture catalogue translated into an image-led city archive
- Visual variance: 7 / 10 — one asymmetric hero, one horizontal archive rail, one sticky narrative sequence
- Motion intensity: 4 / 10 — image reveal, rail feedback, and method-state transitions only
- Information density: 3 / 10 — gallery-like with one dominant idea per viewport
- Asset dependence: 9 / 10 — real Galok photography and the existing film carry the page
- Brand fidelity: 7 / 10 — preserve Galok red, warm paper, seal mark, editorial voice, and site navigation

## System

- Palette: warm paper `#f1eee7`, warm white `#f8f6f1`, ink `#1c1a17`, muted stone `#716b62`, rule `#cbc4b8`, accessible Galok red `#c3312a`
- Typography: Galok English / EB Garamond for display; system grotesk for body and controls
- Spacing: 8px base with 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 96 / 144 steps
- Radius: square editorial surfaces; circular gallery controls only
- Shadows: none on layout surfaces; imagery uses natural contrast instead of elevation
- Motion: `cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1)`, 240–700ms; no parallax, bounce, marquee, or scroll-jacking
