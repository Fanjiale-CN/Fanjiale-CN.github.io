ABOUT / GALOK

Cities are where systems become visible.

Galok is an independent visual archive about cities, consumption, platforms and ordinary life.

视Look closely
Keep the evidence in frame

01 / POSITION

A receipt can hold an economy. A street can show the rules. One photograph can keep a question open.

02 / METHOD

Three working distances.

The archive moves between larger pressure, the frame that makes it readable and the scene where it lands.

VIEW

Follow the pressure.

Policy, price and confidence become meaningful when they change an ordinary decision.

FRAME

Give facts a shape.

Models and visual systems make scattered evidence easier to hold and question.

OBSERVE

Stay with the scene.

Streets, queues, storefronts, coastlines and daily rituals keep abstraction honest.

Editorial direction, web design and moving image.
