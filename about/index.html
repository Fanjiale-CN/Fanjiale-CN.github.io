GALOK / FIELD NOTES VIEW · FRAME · OBSERVE

01 / POSITION

One receipt can hold an economy. One street can show the rules. One photograph can keep a question open.

The archive begins with visible facts, then follows the systems moving behind them. It stays close enough to a scene for abstraction to remain accountable.

02 / WHAT GALOK HOLDS

Four ways into the same archive.
