About Galok Independent / Since 2026

The archive starts where something ordinary refuses to stay ordinary.

A queue outside a store. Rain gathering at a curb. A price that changes a decision. Galok keeps these fragments close enough to see what moves behind them.

It is a personal point of view published under one public identity. The work moves between street photography, editorial design, film and research, with no claim to institutional distance.
