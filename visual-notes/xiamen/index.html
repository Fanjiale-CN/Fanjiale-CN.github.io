Visual Notes / Xiamen

A city as quiet evidence.

Xiamen moves from main gallery to archive. These images can support future writing about city life, tourism, public space, infrastructure and small consumption scenes.

Field note

A coastal city read through edges.

The useful detail in Xiamen is often an edge: water against skyline, campus against tourism, garden shade against heat, old alleys against platform-era check-ins. The archive keeps those edges available for later arguments.

Water Ferries, bridges and skyline views that turn distance into composition. Streets Alleys, storefronts and road curves where the city becomes legible. Leisure Gardens, island walks and pauses that reveal soft consumption.
